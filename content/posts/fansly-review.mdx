## Is Fansly Legit?

Fansly is an established platform with a public Help Center covering payouts, taxes, security and content rules. It issues tax forms to US creators (its help pages cover W-9 and 1099-NEC forms), offers two-factor authentication and explains its chargeback policy in writing. That doesn't guarantee you'll earn anything, but it's how a real business behaves.

As with any platform, the bigger risk usually comes from third parties: fake "management agencies," phishing emails and buyers who want to move off-platform. Keep your login private and only ever sign in through the official site.

## Fansly Fees: How Much Does Fansly Take?

### The 80/20 Split

Fansly is widely reported to pay creators **80%** of revenue from subscriptions, sold media, messages and tips, keeping 20%. Several third-party guides quote this from a Fansly Help Center article on getting started, which they say also mentions "no hidden fees for payouts, processing, or currency conversion."

We couldn't load that wording on an official page ourselves. Fansly's Terms of Service page didn't show readable text to us, and we couldn't find the getting-started article. So we're treating the 80/20 split as **commonly reported**, not confirmed. Check the earnings breakdown in your own Fansly dashboard.

### Pricing Limits

According to Fansly's Help Center:

- **Subscriptions:** "You can set your subscription price anywhere from $5.00 to $499.99 per month."
- **Media sales:** a minimum of $1.00 and a maximum of $499.99 per item.

You can run several subscription tiers with different benefits, or none at all. The Help Center says, "Many successful creators stick to a single subscription tier or none at all!" You can also offer multi-month discounts, public promotions and private offers.

## How Fansly Payouts Work

### The 7-Day Pending Period

Fansly's Help Center article "Where Are My Earnings?" says: "After each transaction, your funds enter a 7-day pending period." Its "Payout Processing Times" page gives an example: "if you earn $5 on the 13th at 1 pm, it becomes available on the 20th at 1 pm."

Your Wallet & Payouts page shows three numbers:

- **Total Balance:** everything, pending and available
- **Pending Balance:** earnings still in the 7-day hold
- **Available for Payout:** what you can withdraw now

There's a SEPA exception: "you can request a payout as low as $20," but if your total doesn't reach $50, it's sent automatically 14 days after your first request.

Fansly also explains **hard and soft limits**. With a hard limit, you can't request below the minimum. With a soft limit, a smaller request stays approved until your approved requests add up to the minimum, then it's sent automatically.

The Help Center's "Requesting a Payout" page says the minimum "for most serviced countries is $20," and "some regions or payout providers may have different minimum thresholds." **Check the official site** for your country.

### Payout Timing

According to the "Payout Processing Times" article in Fansly's Help Center:

- **Approval:** "Most payout requests are reviewed and approved by our team within 48 hours, though often sooner."
- **Delivery:** once a payout is marked Processing, "delivery typically occurs within 1–3 business days."
- **Faster methods:** cryptocurrency, Paxum and Cosmopayment usually arrive "within 24 hours or less."
- **Batching:** international bank transfers and crypto are processed every three days, with a 72-hour cooldown that groups new requests.

The Requesting a Payout page adds that payouts "may take up to four (4) business days to fully process." If a payout takes longer than three business days, or shows Processed but hasn't arrived, Fansly asks you to contact support.

### Chargebacks

The "Your Audience" article in Fansly's Help Center answers "Am I protected from chargebacks?" with "Yes, almost always." It adds that Fansly reserves the right to deduct earnings if your account gets an unusually high number of chargebacks, or if the money came from content that breaks its terms.

### Taxes

Fansly's Help Center says it "does not withhold any taxes from the funds you earn on the platform." You're responsible for reporting and paying tax where you live. Fansly collects W-9 forms from US creators and W-8BEN/DAC7 forms from others.

## Discovery: How Fans Find You On Fansly

Discovery is one of the main reasons creators look at Fansly. According to its Help Center:

- **Profile tags** are private labels that help "the algorithm understand what kind of content you create" and place you in fans' Suggestions.
- **Hashtags** help fans find posts through search and trending topics.
- **The For You page** can show your content. Fansly's guide on recommendations suggests adding a free preview to locked media you want to appear there.
- **Tagging and reposting** let you collaborate with other creators.

These tools help, but they don't replace promotion. Plenty of creators still bring traffic from social media. How much you get from discovery depends on your niche, your content and how active you are.

## Account Security

Fansly offers **two-factor authentication (2FA)**, according to its Help Center article "Securing Account with 2FA." Turn it on, use a unique password and only sign in through the official site. Be wary of anyone who asks for your login, including "agencies."

## Fansly Pros And Cons

### Pros

- **Built-in discovery** through tags, suggestions and the For You page
- **Flexible pricing:** several subscription tiers, $5.00–$499.99 a month, plus locked posts and tips
- **Clear payout documentation** with minimums and timings by method
- **Fast methods** such as crypto, Paxum and Cosmopayment, which usually arrive within 24 hours
- **Two-factor authentication** available

### Cons

- **The 80/20 split isn't on a page we could read**, so confirm it in your dashboard
- **Higher minimums for some methods:** $50 for SEPA and $100 for SWIFT and crypto
- **A 7-day hold** on every transaction
- **No tax withholding**, so you must budget for tax yourself
- **Discovery isn't guaranteed.** You'll likely still need outside promotion

## Who Is Fansly Best For?

Fansly may suit you if:

- You want built-in discovery tools to help new fans find you
- You'd like to offer several subscription tiers
- Fansly supports a payout method that suits your country

It may suit you less if:

- Your only available payout method has a high minimum and you expect low early earnings
- You don't want to manage your own tax
