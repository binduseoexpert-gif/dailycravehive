## A Safer Setup On X (Step By Step)

### 1. Use A Separate Seller Account

Do not mix family photos, coworkers and feet sales on one login. A dedicated account under a stage name keeps leaks smaller. X Terms still bar deceptive impersonation of real people or brands — a persona is fine; pretending to be someone else is not (confirm current Rules on the official site).

### 2. Lock Down Identity Leakage

- Strip EXIF / location data before upload (cover identifying background detail and metadata before you post)
- Hide tattoos, room landmarks and reflections that dox you
- Use a bio email or marketplace inbox, not your legal-name mail
- Turn on two-factor authentication on X and on every payout account

### 3. Mark Sensitive Media

Before posting bare feet sets or anything sexualised, enable the sensitive-media marking that X expects for adult uploads (Wikipedia summary above). Commonly reported path: compose post → media options → mark as sensitive, plus account-level media settings. Confirm in your app.

### 4. Watermark Every Preview

Previews get stolen and resold. Put a semi-opaque handle or storefront URL on each image/video. Keep full-resolution unlocks on the paid platform only.

### 5. Put The Storefront In The Bio

Commonly reported conversion pattern:

1. Bio link → FeetFinder / OnlyFans / other verified page
2. Pinned post explaining what you sell and where to buy
3. Feed posts = teaser only, with "link in bio" — not "Venmo me $20"

## DMs: Where Most Money Gets Lost

X DMs are for questions and routing buyers to checkout — **not** for taking payment. Patterns covered in our [feet pics scams](/feet-pics-scams) guide show up constantly on social apps:

- **Fake payment screenshots** — "I paid, send the album" with an edited bank image
- **"Kindly" advance-fee scripts** — buyer or "agency" asks you to pay a release fee first
- **Chargeback after cash-app payment** — money appears, files send, payment reverses
- **Impersonation** — accounts posing as FeetFinder/OnlyFans support asking for passwords
- **Escape-to-Telegram pressure** — "X is unsafe, pay me on crypto wallet XYZ"

Safer reply template:

> Thanks for the interest — all sales run through my verified page linked in my bio. I do not take payment in DMs.

Phishing that pretends to be X Support or a marketplace should be checked against the FTC's guidance on [recognizing and avoiding phishing scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-recognize-and-avoid-phishing-scams).
