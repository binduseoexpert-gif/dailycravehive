### How The Fee Works On Tips

Under the Terms (**Last updated: October 2026**), a Creator Interaction includes "any other interaction or payment between a User and a Creator's account or Content, including direct messages." OnlyFans' fee "for Creator Interactions is calculated as 20% of the total User Payment and will be deducted from each User Payment." So a $50 tip is a $50 User Payment; you keep $40 before pending-balance and payout timing. Full mechanics are in our [OnlyFans fees and payouts](/onlyfans-fees-payouts) guide.

OnlyFans sets minimum and maximum tip amounts, but the public pages we checked do not list them. Creators **commonly report** a $5 minimum and a lower maximum on newer accounts that rises over time. Confirm the limits in your own tip field before you price a menu item above them — if an item costs more than a fan can tip in one go, sell it as a PPV message instead.

## Tip Menu Vs PPV: Which Payment Route?

A menu item can be paid two ways. A good menu uses both.

| Route | How it works | Best for | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tip | Fan tips the menu price and writes what it's for; you deliver afterwards in DMs | Fan-initiated requests, shout-outs, ratings, customs | Delivery is your promise — keep it |
| PPV message | You send locked media at the menu price; fan unlocks it instantly | Ready-made sets and clips listed on the menu | Preview and caption do the selling |

Both are User Payments at the same 20% fee. Our [OnlyFans PPV messages](/onlyfans-ppv-messages) guide covers pricing and sending locked messages step by step.

## How To Structure A Tip Menu

Build a ladder, not a long list. Five to ten items is a common range; creators **commonly report** that longer menus get skimmed.

1. **An easy first buy.** One very low-priced item (a reply, a rating, a name said in a short clip). Its job is to turn a subscriber into a buyer once.
2. **Your regulars.** Three to five things your page is known for, priced to match the effort.
3. **A bundle.** Two or three regular items combined at a small discount to raise the average purchase.
4. **A custom at the top.** Priced from your time. It anchors everything below it.
5. **A "not offered" line.** One sentence listing what you don't do. It saves your inbox from the same request every day.

### Pricing Your Menu

OnlyFans doesn't publish recommended tip-menu prices. A few principles keep pricing sane:

- **Price from your time.** If a custom takes an hour to plan, shoot and edit, the price has to cover that hour after the 20% fee.
- **Leave gaps between rungs.** Items priced close together make fans pick the cheapest one.
- **Do the 80% math.** A $25 item nets $20 before payout timing.
- **Re-price rather than discount forever.** Raise prices as demand grows; keep promos time-limited.
- **Stay inside tip limits.** If an item is above your maximum single tip, route it through PPV.

## Where To Put Your Tip Menu

- **Pinned post** — the placement OnlyFans names. Every new subscriber sees it first.
- **Welcome message** — mention one easy first-buy item and point to the pinned post. The Features guide notes you can set a welcome message for new subscribers.
- **Bio** — one line saying a tip menu is pinned, not the whole list.
- **Mass DMs** — repost the menu with seasonal or limited-time items.
- **Livestreams** — tie menu items or a tip goal to a stream. All creators who can go live can set a tip goal and optional reward (Go Live guide). See [how to go live on OnlyFans](/onlyfans-live-stream).

Never point fans to off-platform payment for menu items. It breaks the record you need for disputes and taxes and is a common scam setup — see our [OnlyFans scams](/onlyfans-scams) guide.

## Delivering What Fans Pay For

A tip is a payment with a promise attached. To keep fans and avoid disputes:

- **State delivery times** on the menu ("customs within 3 days").
- **Deliver on-platform** in DMs, as media or a message.
- **Keep notes** of what each fan bought and when you delivered.
- **Say no early** to requests outside your menu or the rules, before money moves.

The Terms warn fans against "unjustified chargeback requests" for any "tip to a Creator," and say bad-faith requests can lead to the fan's account being suspended or deleted. If a fan successfully charges back a User Payment, OnlyFans may deduct an amount equal to the Creator Earnings portion. Clear delivery records are your best protection.

## Rules That Still Apply To Menu Items

- **Content rules.** Menu items are Content and Creator Interactions; the Terms and Acceptable Use Policy apply to every one of them.
- **Co-Authored Content.** Anyone else who appears must be a tagged Creator or an 18+ person whose ID and written consent you hold (Terms).
- **No in-person meetups.** The Creator Center says in-real-life meetups between creators and fans are strictly prohibited — never list one.
- **You're responsible.** If someone helps run your account, the Terms say your legal responsibility doesn't change.

## Taxes On Tip Income (US Creators)

Tips are income. US creators should keep their OnlyFans statements and track Form 1099-NEC, which the IRS describes on its [About Form 1099-NEC](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-1099-nec) page. The reporting threshold is **$2,000** for payments made in **2026** ($600 for earlier years). Not tax advice — check with a tax professional.
