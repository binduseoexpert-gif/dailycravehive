## Step 1: Create Your Account

The Creator Hub says to sign up "using your email or streamline the process with your Twitter, Google or Twitch account." You'll get a confirmation email — click the link to activate the account. Then go to **"Become a Model"** in your profile menu, or straight to fansly.com/application.

Pick your username carefully before you apply. Creators **commonly report** that changing a username after approval requires a support ticket.

## Step 2: The Ondato Identity Check

Fansly says: "For our community's safety, you must verify your identity through Ondato." Ondato is a third-party identity-verification service — the same kind of [know-your-customer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Know_your_customer) check banks and payment apps use. You'll need:

- Your ID — "passport, identity card or driver's license"
- "A well-lit spot to take clear photos of your ID and a selfie"

Fansly's pro tip: use "your phone or digital camera instead of your computer's camera." It says photos "need to be sharp, clear, and well-lit to speed up your verification process."

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Use your phone's rear camera | Use a laptop webcam |
| Shoot in even daylight or bright indoor light | Shoot under a single lamp with glare |
| Keep all four corners of the ID in frame | Crop the edges |
| Use a current, undamaged ID | Use an expired or cracked ID |
| Take the selfie with your face fully visible | Wear sunglasses or a hat |

## Step 3: Finish Your Fansly Application

Once Ondato is done, Fansly says "you must finish your application with us" at fansly.com/application. The Creator Hub lists three items:

1. "Upload photos of both the front and back of your ID."
2. "Snap a photo with your ID and a handwritten note stating your name, today's date, and 'For Fansly'."
3. "Record a video showing your ID's front and back, with your face visible, and clearly say your full name and 'For Fansly'."

### The Handwritten Note

This is the step people miss most. The note needs:

- Your name (use your **legal name** exactly as it appears on the ID)
- Today's date
- The words **"For Fansly"**

Hold the note and the ID together in one photo, with your face visible.

### The Video

Keep it short and simple:

- Show the front of your ID, then the back
- Keep your face in frame
- Say your full name and "For Fansly" clearly

Third-party guides **commonly report** that Fansly's Help Center words this slightly differently (for example, holding the sign in the video too). To cover both versions, hold the ID and the note on camera and say the words.

## Why Fansly Applications Get Delayed Or Rejected

Fansly doesn't publish a list of rejection reasons. What we can say is what the official page asks for — and missing any of it is the most likely cause of a delay. Based on the Creator Hub and **commonly reported** creator experiences:

| Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|
| Blurry, dark or glare-heavy photos | Retake with a phone camera in even light |
| Cropped ID | Show all four corners |
| Missing handwritten note | Write name, today's date and "For Fansly" |
| Name on note doesn't match ID | Use your legal name exactly as on the ID |
| Video doesn't show both sides of the ID | Show front, then back, with your face in frame |
| Not saying your full name and "For Fansly" | Say both clearly in the video |
| Expired or damaged ID | Use a current, undamaged ID |
| Payout details in a different name | **Commonly reported**: payout account name should match your ID |
| Email went to spam | Add applications@fansly.com to safe senders |

### Name Changes And Older IDs

If your ID shows a former name or an older photo, creators **commonly report** that contacting support before submitting saves time. Ask which supporting documents (for example, name-change paperwork) they'll accept.

## What To Do If You're Rejected Or Stuck

1. **Check spam first.** Fansly says to make sure "no emails from us have landed in your spam folder."
2. **Try a different browser** and check your connection — both are on Fansly's own support checklist.
3. **Wait the full 10 business days** before emailing, per the Creator Hub.
4. **Email from your account address** with a short, calm note and corrected photos if needed.
5. **Don't open a second account** to try again — creators **commonly report** this restarts the whole process and can complicate review.

Be wary of anyone who offers to "get you verified fast" for a fee. Fansly's Creator Hub lists no verification fee — the process runs through Ondato and fansly.com/application. ## Special Cases: Couples, Agencies And Virtual Creators

The Creator Hub lists three special cases:

- **Couples:** "One person will be the account holder and receive payouts. However, you'll both need to submit consent documents."
- **Agencies:** "One person will be the account owner, but each creator must be documented and provide consent."
- **Virtual entities:** "Virtual creators should register using their creator persona but provide legal identification for verification purposes."
