## What Is An OnlyFans PPV Message?

The OnlyFans Creator Center says "Pay-Per-View messages, posts, and streams allow your fans to pick and choose which of your content they'll pay to see." In the Terms of Service, a payment for pay-per-view Content is one type of **Creator Interaction**, alongside subscriptions and other payments between a fan and a creator, including direct messages.

A **PPV message** is a direct message with a price on the attached media. The fan sees your text (and any free preview you add) and pays to unlock the rest. The Creator Center puts it simply: "To unlock a PPV message, a subscriber will first need to pay the price you've set in advance." That payment is separate from their subscription. The Terms even define a Subscription as access to a creator's content "excluding pay-per-view content."

OnlyFans also supports:

- **PPV posts** (paid posts on your feed). The Creator Center calls these "a great way for free accounts to monetize selectively."
- **PPV streams**, which work like ticketed live events, for free accounts that are eligible to go live (see [how to go live on OnlyFans](/onlyfans-live-stream))

This article focuses on **PPV messages**, because every creator can use them. Posts and streams get a short section near the end.

## Free Account vs Paid Account

The Creator Center draws a clear line between the two setups:

- **Paid subscription accounts** put your posts, stories and streams behind a monthly price. PPV messages still work as extras: customs, longer videos or one-off drops.
- **Free accounts** don't charge a monthly fee. Fans subscribe for free, then pay for what they want. The Creator Center says free accounts "have access to pay-per-view posts and streams, and can require fans to tip first before sending a direct message." It adds: "For free profiles, using PPVs effectively is crucial to maximizing monetization on OnlyFans."

Neither model is "better" on paper. The Creator Center says paid subscriptions can make earnings "more consistent and predictable," while free accounts "on average" gain subscribers more quickly and lean harder on PPV and tips. ## How To Send A PPV Message

OnlyFans' official instructions are short. The Features guide says: "Simply name your price, attach your media, and press send," and you can target individual subscribers or all your fans at once.

The Creator Center describes the steps like this:

1. Open your messages
2. Select the fans you want to target (or choose "all subscribers" to reach everyone)
3. Write a description of the PPV content
4. Attach the media
5. Set your price
6. Send

The Features guide's Mass DMs section matches that flow: start a New Message, select fans to include or exclude, draft the message, attach media or PPV content, and send.

Creators commonly report that you can also pull media straight from the Vault, add a free preview teaser and schedule the send for later. Treat those interface details as **commonly reported** and confirm them in your own app, because menus change.

**Lists help targeting.** The Features guide says Lists let you organize your fans into groups and target them for messaging, promotions and live streams. A commonly reported approach is to send cheaper welcome PPVs to new fans and higher-priced customs to top spenders, rather than blasting everyone the same offer.

## How To Price OnlyFans PPV

OnlyFans doesn't publish a recommended dollar amount for every niche. Here's what the Creator Center does say:

- "When experimenting with pricing, remember that it's usually better to start low and work your way up."
- PPV should be "reserved for your most exclusive content that your fans won't find anywhere else."
- "If you've already posted something for free on OnlyFans or other social media, it's probably not a great idea to offer it as a PPV."
- "If you're not sure what PPV content your fans will be willing to pay for, try asking them directly."

The Terms back this up: creators are "solely responsible for determining any pricing" for Creator Interactions. So the official framework is exclusive content, start low, ask your audience and raise prices as you learn.

Exact minimum and maximum prices for paid messages and posts are **commonly reported** by creators and third-party guides (often a few dollars at the low end and around $100 at the high end for messages). We couldn't load OnlyFans' Help Center article with those limits, so **check the official site** or the price field in your composer for the current range. Don't copy a number from a blog that may be out of date.

## Mass Messages, Welcome Messages And Top Fans

PPV works best when the right fans see the right offer.

- **Mass DMs.** Per the Features guide: New Message, include or exclude fans, attach PPV, send. Good for announcements and timed drops.
- **Automatic welcome message.** The Features guide says you set this under Chats in your Settings. Creators commonly report attaching a low-priced PPV or a free teaser here. Confirm the option in your own settings.
- **Top Fans.** The Features guide says OnlyFans works out your top 1–5% of subscribers by what they've spent on subscriptions, tips, pay-per-view content and other methods, and you can send them an exclusive message. That's a natural place for higher-priced customs, not a generic blast.
- **Polls.** Ask what fans want next, as long as the poll follows the Acceptable Use Policy (Features guide).

## After You Send: Vault And Statistics

**Vault.** The Features guide says the Vault stores previously posted or scheduled photos, videos and live streams, and also keeps content you've already shared with subscribers over DM. In its words, if you forgot which content you've sent out over messages or PPV, "the Vault will remember for you," so you don't resell the same file as "new."

**Statistics.** The Creator Center's statistics pages show earnings by type, including messages, plus engagement such as views, likes, tips and purchases, and where your visitors come from. Together they tell you which PPV sends made money and which only got opens. Check your own numbers rather than industry "average unlock rate" claims, which are almost always **commonly reported** and unverified.

## PPV Posts And Streams (Free Accounts)

If you run a free account, the Creator Center also covers:

- **PPV posts:** attach media to a post, set a price and write a caption that makes fans curious enough to unlock it.
- **PPV streams:** think of them "like selling tickets to a live event." The Creator Center says you can "set your ticket prices as low as $5." Live streaming isn't available on day one. OnlyFans adds the Go Live option once your profile has been running for a while and you have a history of content that follows the Terms.

## Safety And Scam Watchouts

- **Watermark before you scale.** Turn on watermarking in your privacy and safety settings (Creator Center) before sending PPV to large lists. A file sent to hundreds of fans is far easier to leak.
- **Customs still need co-author rules.** If anyone else appears in your Content, the Terms (Section 16) require you to tag them as a creator, or hold their proof of age and identity and their written consent. Missing paperwork can lead to Content or account restrictions and withheld earnings.
- **You're responsible for your DMs.** The Terms say that if a third party helps manage your account, you're still legally responsible for everything sent from it. Hand over PPV sending only to people you trust, and read our guide to [OnlyFans chatters](/onlyfans-chatters) before you hire one.
- **Fake unlock bots and agency pitches.** Anyone promising guaranteed unlock rates, or asking for your login to "run your PPV," is a red flag. Our OnlyFans scams guide covers common patterns, and the FTC's page on [recognizing and avoiding phishing scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-recognize-and-avoid-phishing-scams) is a useful refresher.
