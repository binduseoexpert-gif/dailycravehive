## Fanvue Overview: The Challenger

Fanvue is a subscription platform run by **Shift Holdings Ltd**, a UK company. It was founded in 2020 and grew quickly by pitching itself as the "AI-forward" alternative. By its own marketing, it hosts **200,000+ creators** and has paid out **$500M+**. Those are Fanvue's figures, not independently audited ones, but they put it well past the "random new site" stage. Creators sell subscriptions (priced $3.99–$100/month), paywalled posts ($3–$500 each), pay-to-view messages, tips, bundles, and checkout links.

### Fanvue fees: read the intro-rate small print

This is where a lot of 2026 comparisons, older ones included, get it wrong:

- **Standard rate: 20% fee, you keep 80%.** That's written into Fanvue's [Creator Earnings & Payouts policy](https://legal.fanvue.com/creator-earnings-payouts) as the default.
- **Intro rate: 15% fee, you keep 85%, for a short window.** Fanvue's January 2026 blog says the 15% applies "for your first 30 days after you pass KYC." Its creator signup page says "85% in month one, 80% after." One of its own OnlyFans-comparison landing pages goes even shorter and says "first week."
- **The policy lets Fanvue change promos at will.** The legal text says promotional rates "may be introduced, updated, extended, reduced, or withdrawn." The 85% rate given to the 2022 cohort has already been moved to 80%, according to Fanvue's help centre.

You'll still see "85% for 12 months" in third-party guides. Treat that as outdated or limited to earlier groups of sign-ups. **Plan your budget on 80%** and count anything extra as a bonus. Check the rate shown in your own dashboard when you sign up.

### Fanvue's selling point: tools, not just the fee

The more lasting difference is the toolkit: automated messages triggered by fan actions, AI-assisted messaging, AI voice notes and voice calls, and AI earnings analytics. Fanvue also allows **AI-generated and virtual creators**, as long as they follow its labelling rules. Human creators can use the messaging tools to save time, but you'll also be competing with accounts that never log off. We dig into this in our full [Fanvue review](/fanvue-review).

## OnlyFans Overview: The Incumbent

OnlyFans is operated by **Fenix International Limited**, registered in England and Wales. It's the category's household name. For a lot of fans, "subscription content" simply means OnlyFans.

### OnlyFans fees: one number, no promos

OnlyFans' Terms of Service put it plainly: the fee "is calculated as **20% of the total Fan Payment**." That covers subscriptions, tips, PPV, and every other revenue stream. There's no intro discount and no volume tier. It's a flat 20% from your first dollar to your millionth.

### Why the brand matters more than the fee

The value of OnlyFans isn't its toolset. It's familiarity. A cold follower who clicks your link already knows what they're buying, and many already have a payment method saved on the platform. That cuts checkout friction, and checkout friction quietly kills conversions. You can't put a number on it, but creators who move established audiences off OnlyFans often feel it.

### The real fee gap, in dollars

Say you gross $1,500 in your first month on each platform:

- **Fanvue (15% intro):** you keep $1,275
- **OnlyFans (20%):** you keep $1,200

That's a $75 difference, **once**. From month two onward, both keep 20%. Over a full year at that pace, Fanvue's fee edge adds up to the same $75. Nice to have, but it shouldn't decide where you build your business.

### Two Fanvue payout rules worth knowing

Fanvue's published policy includes two details beginners miss. First, if your chargeback/dispute rate goes above **1.5% in a rolling 30 days**, Fanvue may charge a **$50 admin fee per extra dispute**. Second, a balance you leave untouched for **12 months** can be classed as "dormant." Withdraw regularly, and keep payment on-platform so disputes stay low.

## Discovery and Traffic: Who Brings the Buyers?

This matters more than the fee table.

**OnlyFans brings almost no one to you.** There's no real browse feed that puts unknown creators in front of fans. Your income depends on traffic you send from Reddit, X, TikTok, Instagram, or niche communities. What OnlyFans does well is *convert* that traffic, because the brand is trusted.

**Fanvue sits in the middle.** It markets itself as more social, and creators report *some* in-app visibility. It isn't a strong discovery engine, though, and Fanvue's own guide describes the platform as a "home base" while you "keep posting on socials to reach new people." In other words, even the challenger expects you to bring your own buyers.

> **The honest read:** neither platform replaces off-platform promotion. Fanvue gives a new account a slightly better chance of being seen. OnlyFans gives an established account a better chance of a fan actually paying.

## Which One Should You Pick?

### New creators (zero to small following)

**Lean Fanvue.** The month-one discount helps a little while you work out pricing, the messaging automation saves time, and the $20 minimum (once you have 5 uploads) means early earnings aren't stuck. Get verified on day one so the intro window doesn't run out during paperwork. Then promote off-platform every day.

### Established creators (real traffic already)

**Stay on OnlyFans as your primary.** If you're already sending a steady flow of followers, a one-off 5% saving won't make up for even a small drop in conversions. Add Fanvue as a second storefront instead of migrating.

### Niche, chat-heavy, or AI-persona creators

**Fanvue is the natural fit.** It openly hosts AI and virtual creators and builds its tooling around conversation-driven sales. For human creators in narrow niches, AI-assisted DMs can make a small, high-spending audience manageable. Your edge over AI accounts is being a real person, so don't automate that away.

## Can You Use Both? (The Cross-Promotion Play)

Yes. Neither platform's terms stop you from running the other, and two storefronts protect you if one platform changes its rules or has a payment hiccup.

A simple setup that works:

1. **Give each platform a role.** OnlyFans is the link in your bios for warm, ready-to-buy traffic. Fanvue is for chat-heavy fans and new audiences you reach through promo.
2. **Stagger releases.** Post sets on one platform a day or two before the other, so subscribers on both still feel they're getting fresh content.
3. **Keep pricing consistent.** Big price gaps teach fans to wait for the cheaper version. Small, deliberate differences are fine.
4. **Track revenue per fan, not follower counts.** After 60 days, put more effort into whichever platform earns more per hour you spend on it.
5. **Never move payment off-platform** to "save fees." That's how chargebacks, scams, and stolen content start.

The real cost is two inboxes. Fanvue's automation softens that, but plan your time honestly.

## Conclusion: The Verdict

Fanvue's real edge is its tooling: automated and AI-assisted messaging, voice features, and analytics built for creators who earn through conversation.
