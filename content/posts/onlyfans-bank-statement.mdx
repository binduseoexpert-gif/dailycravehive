## Who Bills You: Fenix And OnlyFans

OnlyFans' Terms say the platform is operated by **Fenix International Limited**, a UK company (registration number 10354575, Cheapside, London). That same entity is listed on the UK [Companies House](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/10354575) register and as the parent on [Wikipedia's OnlyFans page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OnlyFans).

For fans, that means the merchant behind a subscription, tip or pay-per-view unlock is the **platform company**, not the individual creator. User Payments are received and processed by a payment provider OnlyFans approves (Terms, Creator Earnings section). Source: OnlyFans Terms of Service, Last updated: October 2026. Your bank prints whatever merchant descriptor that processor and your card network send — which is why two fans can see slightly different text for the same kind of purchase.

## What Fans Commonly See On Statements

OnlyFans does not publish an official table of billing descriptors. Across creator forums, bank apps and consumer write-ups, fans **commonly report** lines such as:

- **ONLYFANS** or **ONLYFANS.COM** (sometimes with a letter suffix)
- **FENIX INTERNATIONAL**, **FENIX INTL**, or similar truncations
- **FENIX INTERNET LLC** (a US-related entity name some US cards show)
- Processor-style lines that still include OnlyFans (for example formats that pair a processor brand with OnlyFans)

Treat every specific string as **commonly reported**. Your issuer may shorten, translate or reformat the merchant name. Search your bank's own transaction detail for the full merchant field if the app preview looks cryptic.

### What Usually Does *Not* Appear

Creators and fans commonly report that **individual creator usernames do not print** on the card statement. Older anecdotes about "OF*creatorname" style lines circulate online; treat those as historical or unverified unless you see them on your own statement. The reliable privacy fact is still limited: the platform is identifiable even when the creator is not.

## Recurring Charges, Holds And Wallet Credits

From the official Terms:

- Selecting **Subscribe** starts a Subscription that **automatically renews** at the current rate unless you cancel auto-renew, the price rises, payment fails without a backup method, or you close the account before renewal.
- The Terms state that **all Subscriptions are non-refundable** and that you **may not be Notified** of a Subscription renewal. You are charged at each renewal unless payment fails after retries with no backup method, the price increased, you cancel the Subscription, or you closed your OnlyFans account before renewal. Source: OnlyFans Terms of Service, Last updated: October 2026.
- **Wallet Credits** are prepaid balances for User Payments. They are non-refundable. If a purchase costs more than remaining credits, your payment method is charged the **full** purchase amount (credits are not split across part of that buy). Source: same Terms.
- Displayed local-currency amounts **may include currency conversion fees and processing costs**. Payout conversions also include conversion fees. Source: same Terms.

Fans commonly report a small authorisation or verification amount when adding a card. Amounts and whether they reverse vary by bank — confirm in your own app.

## Chargebacks: What Fans And Creators Should Know

### If You Are A Fan

Disagreeing with a charge is a normal banking right, but OnlyFans' Terms warn against **unjustified** refund or chargeback requests. If OnlyFans determines a request was made in **bad faith**, it may **suspend or delete** your User account. Source: OnlyFans Terms of Service, Last updated: October 2026.

For genuine unauthorised charges, contact your card issuer promptly and use OnlyFans' official support channels. Watch for phishing emails that claim to "remove a statement line" for a fee — that is not how banks work. The FTC's guide to [recognizing and avoiding phishing scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-recognize-and-avoid-phishing-scams) is a useful refresher. Our [OnlyFans scams](/onlyfans-scams) guide covers creator- and fan-facing cons.

### If You Are A Creator

When a Fan successfully seeks a refund or chargeback of a User Payment, the Terms say OnlyFans may **deduct** an amount equal to the Creator Earnings portion of that amount. Reviews can suspend the account, pause User Payments and withhold earnings when OnlyFans thinks you have or may have breached the Terms, for reputational-harm / loss risk, or if you do not promptly provide requested information — details in our [account suspended](/onlyfans-account-suspended) guide. Source: OnlyFans Terms of Service, Last updated: October 2026.

Keep your own Statements screenshots and payout records. US tax reporting (including Form 1099-NEC at **$2,000** for payments made in 2026, or $600 before) is separate from what fans see on their cards. See our OnlyFans taxes guide for reporting thresholds. Not tax advice.

## Can You Make OnlyFans "Discreet" On A Statement?

Short answer: **OnlyFans does not advertise a hidden merchant name.** If someone sells "invisible OnlyFans billing" for an upfront fee, treat it as a scam until proven otherwise.

Practical options fans commonly use (none of these are OnlyFans features):

- **Pay with a card only you monitor** — a separate card or account reduces household surprises
- **Virtual cards** from your bank or a reputable card issuer — some mask or replace merchant text; others still pass OnlyFans through. Read your issuer's rules
- **Cancel auto-renew** in the OnlyFans account before you need the subscription to end, rather than waiting for a surprise renewal
- **Privacy on the creator side** — geoblocking and careful promotion help who finds the page, not what the bank prints. See [OnlyFans geoblocking](/onlyfans-geoblocking)

Do not share card details with "billing fixers," and do not dispute a charge you authorised just to hide it — that can close your Fan account and hurt the creator.

## For Creators Explaining Charges To Fans

A calm pinned FAQ saves support time:

- Charges come from **OnlyFans / Fenix**, not from your personal name
- Amounts are in **USD** plus any tax or conversion your bank adds
- Renewals continue until the fan turns off auto-renew or cancels
- How to cancel on-platform (point to OnlyFans' own help, not a third-party "hack")

Never ask fans to lie to their bank. That creates chargebacks you may repay.
