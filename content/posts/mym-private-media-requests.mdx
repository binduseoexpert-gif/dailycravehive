A MYM private media request is a native transaction, not a tip and not a pre-made unlock sent to a list. Official MYM help says a subscriber can request private photos or videos, you can accept or refuse, and if you accept you enter a price in a dedicated field. If the subscriber accepts that proposal, official English help says you must transmit the requested content "as quickly as possible (72 hours maximum) through MYM messaging." Your account is then "automatically credited with the amount entered upon media delivery."

That is a different workflow from an OnlyFans mass PPV message or a pinned tip menu. Our [OnlyFans PPV messages](/onlyfans-ppv-messages) guide covers locked library unlocks in DMs. An OnlyFans tip menu is offer design, not a platform-managed quote the fan has to accept. This page stays on MYM's official request, quote, acceptance, and delivery steps, and on the rule that the media must match the request.

We checked MYM's official English help articles ["Private medias"](https://support.mym.fans/hc/en-us/articles/360013758320-Private-medias) and ["Introduction to MYM"](https://support.mym.fans/hc/en-us/articles/360013830159-Introduction-to-MYM) on November 19, 2026, and re-checked them on November 22, 2026. Official French-language fan help, ["Média privé"](https://support.mym.fans/hc/fr/articles/12676244116252-Media-prive) (first published 16 February 2024, last edited March 9, 2026), still describes a 72-hour delivery window after the fan has accepted and paid. Official help does not publish a MYM fee percentage in these articles, so this guide does not turn a credited amount into a take-home figure. ## Find The Request And Decide

Official help starts with a notification, not with a store listing. "You will be notified by email as soon as a subscriber requests private media." Then: "You will find all requests for photos or videos in the section: My messages."

From there, official help gives you two official responses: "accepting and mentioning your price in the dedicated field," or "refusing." Official English help does not describe a third official response, a built-in counter-offer tool, or a way to change the price after the fan has already received your proposal, so this article does not invent those controls. If the current conversation shows a different label than "My messages" or a different price field name, use what the screen shows and treat the official wording as the help-center description.

Refusing is part of the official flow. Official help does not require you to accept every request. It also does not publish a response-time limit for the accept-or-refuse step, so do not assume a deadline until the current page or conversation states one.

## Deliver The Exact Media, Within 72 Hours

Once the fan accepts the proposal, official English help says: "you will have to transmit the requested content as quickly as possible (72 hours maximum) through MYM messaging." Official help does not state which timezone that clock uses, or where a countdown appears, so check the time or timer shown in the current conversation before you treat 72 hours as a local-clock promise.

Delivery is through "MYM messaging." Official help does not describe an official way to complete this particular paid request by posting the media to your feed, sending it as a Story, or handing it over on another app. Keep the file in the official messaging flow unless the current screen clearly offers a different official delivery control for that request.

The matching rule is the part official help puts in its own box, and it is worth quoting in full:

> It is essential to deliver the media that exactly matches the user's request, and not a random media while waiting for the requested content to be produced. If the delivered media does not match the description, we reserve the right to refund the fan and deduct the earnings associated with this purchase.

That means a placeholder clip, a teaser, or a different file sent "while you finish the real one" is not what official help asks for. If you cannot produce the requested media in time, official English help does not publish a creator-side extension button or a missed-deadline script. It also does not say what happens to the request if 72 hours pass with no delivery. Do not assume a specific penalty beyond the written refund-and-deduction rule for media that does not match the description. If you need a decision on a delay, official help is the place to ask, rather than sending unmatched media to "stop the clock."

The same article ends with a rights reminder that applies to anything you send on MYM: "We remind you that you can only post on MYM content for which you have distribution rights. Sending a media that you do not have the rights will be punished." This guide is not legal advice. If a request would require someone else's image, a trademark, or a co-performer you cannot lawfully include, official help's rights line is a reason to refuse rather than to improvise.

When the matching media is delivered, official help says your account is "automatically credited with the amount entered." That is the amount you typed in the dedicated field, not a promise of what you will withdraw after any platform or payment-provider fees. These request articles do not publish a fee percentage. ## This Is Not A Group Push Or A Tip Menu

Official help also describes a different button at the bottom of a subscriber conversation: **"Offer a private push."** You can use it to "propose yourself a private paid media to your subscriber," or, in the free-media section, to "add a media to send free to your subscriber." That is a creator-started send, not the incoming request flow this article covers.

Official help's group tools (Group Messages, Stories) are also separate. This guide does not walk through group pushes, visibility after a subscription ends, or Story audiences. Those are different official features, and official help treats them in their own sections.
