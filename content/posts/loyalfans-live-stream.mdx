## Open Go Live And Set Camera, Mic And Audience

Official help gives two official entry points. "The first location is on top of your timeline, click on the fourth tab to access it." The same article says Go Live is "also located at the top of the main dashboard. Locate Go Live under your avatar and click on it." If your current dashboard or timeline uses a different tab order or icon, confirm what is on screen rather than assuming the 2023 "fourth tab" label is unchanged.

Before you start, official help says to select the camera and microphone you will use. "At the top left you will find two black round icons. The Microphone on the left are the drop down options for your sound." "The Camera on the right are the drop down options for your camera." Official help says you can use "the default internal microphone or camera built in your laptop or computer or external ones, using Bluetooth or [OBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OBS_Studio)."

That is the extent of the official OBS instruction in these articles. Official help does not publish a stream server, a stream key, or an OBS setup sequence. This guide will not invent those steps or show a key. If the current camera dropdown reveals more OBS options than the help article names, use what the screen shows and keep any key private.

Next, official help says to select your viewing audience before you start. "At the bottom left of your screen you will find your viewing audience drop down: Who Can Watch: Public/Followers/Subscriber." You can change that setting during the show among Public, Followers, and Subscribers. Official help does not define those three groups beyond the labels, and it does not say what a "Public" listing shows to someone who is not logged in. Choose the first audience, then confirm it again if you change it mid-show.

## Set Up To Three Tip Goals

Official help calls tip goals "one great feature" of Go Live: "Letting your fans know three goals you would like to reach during your live cam show using their tips as incentive." Open them with "the Trophy icon at the top right of your screen." Official help names the window **COUNTDOWN GOAL** boards and describes three levels:

- Level 1: topic and total price
- Level 2: topic and total price
- Level 3: topic and total price

There is also "a MINIMUM TIP TO PARTICIPATE field where you can enter the minimum amount in US dollars to participate at each Level." Official help treats the total price and the minimum tip as separate fields. It does not publish a required amount, a platform maximum, or a suggested target, so this article does not invent rates. A goal is a show feature, not a promise of income.

After you edit the goals, official help says to click the green **CONFIRM** button to save. Official help also says you can change this during the show: "Once a Level Goal has been reached with tips. The GO LIVE Creator can decide to block this level to only the viewers who have tipped. The levels can also be reset and modified throughout the entire GO LIVE Cam show." If you restrict a completed level, official help does not say that everyone who was already watching still has access, so tell viewers what the current level requires.

## Start The Broadcast, Then Use Chat, Whispers And Blocks

Settings and audience come first. Official help is explicit about the listing: "You will not be listed in the GO LIVE general LoyalFans listing until you click on the Bottom Red Button: START BROADCAST." After that, "You are now LIVE and viewers can enter your chatroom and watch your GO LIVE Show."

You can talk with the microphone. To type, official help says to use the **LIVE CHAT** tab and the field labelled "Write your message here." To see viewers, open the **USERS** tab. Official help says you can select a viewer and send a **WHISPER**, "a private DM that the general audience won't see," and it describes whispers as a way "to encourage ONLY PRIVATE SHOWS."

To block someone during the show, official help says to select them on the **USERS** tab. "Your blocked users will then be listed under the BLOCKED USERS Tab and won't be able to see your GO LIVE show until you stop the current live show and start a new one." Official help describes this as a show-level block and does not say it carries into the next broadcast.

To stop, official help says to click the bottom middle red **STOP** button. "This will end your Live show and you will no longer be listed in the active general LIVE CONTENT CREATORS listing for live shows." Official help also says "you will also hear a voice recording 'EPIC' that confirms that you have stopped your GO LIVE cam show."

## Only Private And The Per-Minute Price

**ONLY PRIVATE** is a separate paid room inside the same show, not a new listing. Official help says it is "an option where viewers can pay per minute to watch an exclusive show or part of the show." "ONLY PRIVATE is only visible to the viewers who have clicked on this option. Once someone goes private that portion of the show will only be visible to those who are in the ONLY PRIVATE viewing room." To enter that mode, official help says to click the bottom left red **ONLY PRIVATE** button.

Per-minute pricing has two official places. In the Go Live settings wheel, official help lists **PRIVATE PRICE**: "Enter your price per minute for your ONLY PRIVATE SHOWS requests." The same wheel has **Auto Accept Private Requests**, which you can toggle on or off. "If this feature is not turned ON then you will need to manually accept the ONLY PRIVATE SHOWS requests." Official help also has a dashboard default: "The SETTINGS / STREAM section allows you to set the default private price for all streams in the GO LIVE Feature. For Go Private Shows." Official help does not publish a required per-minute amount in these articles. Only Private is still part of a one-to-many show. For a paid two-way call with a single fan, see our guide to [LoyalFans video calls](/loyalfans-video-calls), which covers One on One.

The settings wheel also includes **Mirror My video**, a toggle official help describes as changing "the Horizontal view of your camera." Official help does not say this changes what is recorded or what viewers on other devices see, so confirm the preview before you treat it as a finished framing tool.

## Record The Show, Then Decide Where The File Goes

Official help says you can record a Go Live cam show. To start, click the bottom left red **START RECORD** button. Official help says a message appears in the top right corner: "Live recorded Video." To stop, click **STOP RECORD**. Another message appears: "Live Recorded video," and "The Video is being processed and will be available in Live recorded videos gallery."

Recordings live in **LIVE RECORDED VIDEOS** on the main dashboard. Official help says each recording "can be downloaded or embedded in a timeline post by clicking the red button: POST IT NOW. The recording can also be deleted." If you download a recording, official help says you can "send them by DM to your Fans and Subscribers for free or as locked messages or add them to your video store." The store listing itself is covered in our LoyalFans video store guide.

Official help does not say whether a recording includes an Only Private segment, how long processing takes, or what quality you get. Confirm the file in LIVE RECORDED VIDEOS before you post, download, or add it to the store, especially if part of the show was Only Private.
