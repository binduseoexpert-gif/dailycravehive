## What A Video Store Clip Is (And Isn't)

A video-store clip is a **standalone purchase**. Help describes a listing with its own title, tags, preview, price and optional download. Our LoyalFans review notes that you can sell these clips even to non-subscribers, which is the main difference from a subscription-only post.

This is not:

- A walkthrough of LoyalFans payouts or the platform fee (that's in the review)
- A ManyVids or Clips4Sale store comparison
- A promise that tags or a preview will make the clip sell

Official help does say tags help fans find clips in "the general listing of the LoyalFans video clip stores," and that "show preview" is "recommended for discoverability." That's LoyalFans' own recommendation, not a sales forecast.

## Step 1: Open Add Video To My Video Store

Official help: click the gray button bar at the top of the screen, **ADD VIDEO TO MY VIDEO STORE**. A new window opens. If your layout looks different, look for the same label in the current dashboard. The 2023 help article still uses that exact wording.

Set store-wide defaults first if you want every new clip to start with the same download setting or price (see Settings, later). You can still change those on the individual listing.

## Step 2: Title, Description And Tags

Help lists three fields, in this order:

1. **VIDEO TITLE**: up to 150 characters.
2. **VIDEO DESCRIPTION**: up to 2,000 characters.
3. **ADD TAG**: a maximum of 10 tags.

LoyalFans calls tags important and says they shouldn't be skipped. When a fan searches the general clip section, "videos with that tag will be selected and shown to the fan." Help's own examples include "foot, fetish, domination, femdom, toes, socks." Use tags that honestly describe the clip. Misleading tags can confuse buyers and waste a slot in the 10-tag cap.

## Step 4: Upload The File

Help gives two sources:

- **+ Browse**: a file from your device (phone, tablet, laptop, computer or hard drive)
- **Yman Cloud**: a file already in your media cloud

If the clip started as a Go Live show, official help says you can download it from LIVE RECORDED VIDEOS first; our guide on [how to go live on LoyalFans](/loyalfans-live-stream) covers recording.

Maximum size for the uploaded video, per the help article: **6 GB**.

Confirm the size you see in the current picker. If a file is larger than the form allows, compress or split it before you start; the help article does not describe a workaround.

## Step 5: Cover And Preview

After the file is selected, choose how the clip is previewed. Help lists three modes:

- **Show preview**: the default, "recommended for discoverability of your video clips in the general video clip listing"
- **Show single frame**
- **Show locked screen**

Then you can set a **custom cover** and a **custom preview**.

**Cover options** (official help):

- Upload a custom cover from your device
- Select a custom cover from the media cloud
- Select a frame from the right-hand selection, taken from your clip

**Custom preview options:**

- Upload a custom preview video from your device
- Select a custom preview video from the media cloud

Maximum size for the preview video, per the help article: **500 MB and/or 90 seconds**.

A preview is not a guarantee that the clip will appear higher in search or that it will sell. It is the trailer buyers see before they pay. Keep it accurate; a preview that doesn't match the paid file is a fast way to invite refunds and complaints.

## Step 6: Price, Optional Schedule And Discounts

Help says to set the purchase price in the **PRICE** field after the file, cover and preview are in place. It does not publish a required minimum or maximum in that article, so enter a price the current field accepts and check your dashboard if a limit appears.

### Schedule A Future Release

If you want the clip live as soon as possible, help says: **"DO NOT use this feature."**

To delay the release: under **SCHEDULED VIDEO**, click **Post it later**. A window opens with a **date** tab and a **time** tab. Select both, then click the red **OK**. The listing will show the date and time you picked. Official help does not state which timezone the picker uses, so confirm what's shown in the current window before you rely on a go-live time.

### Optional Per-Clip Discount

You do not have to add a discount. If you do:

1. Choose **FOR** whom: **SUBSCRIBERS**, **FOLLOWERS**, **GROUP** or **INDIVIDUAL**.
2. Choose **DISCOUNT PERCENTAGE**: help says you can select from **5% all the way to 70% OFF**.
3. Optionally set an **END DATE** (date and time) for a limited offer.
4. Click the red **CONFIRM**. The discount appears at the bottom of the add-clip window.

A 70% off price is still a sale. Don't treat the top of that range as a recommended starting point.

## Step 7: Post To Video Store

Official last step: click the red button at the bottom, **POST TO VIDEO STORE**.

That adds the clip to your store, unless you scheduled it for later. After it goes live, keep a record of the price, download setting and any discount so you can answer buyer questions from the listing you actually published.

## Store-Wide Defaults (Settings → Video Store)

The "Settings Video Store Overview" article (updated August 20, 2023) describes defaults for **all** clips, which you can still change on each new upload.

Two tabs:

### General

- **DEFAULT DOWNLOAD OPTION**: toggle download on or off for new videos
- **DEFAULT VIDEO PRICE**: a default price for all videos
- **MASS ACTION: ENABLE DOWNLOAD OPTION FOR ALL VIDEOS**: Apply turns download on for every posted store video
- **MASS ACTION: DISABLE DOWNLOAD OPTION FOR ALL VIDEOS**: Apply turns download off for every posted store video

Mass actions change **existing** listings, not just future ones. Read the button twice before you click Apply.

### Discounts

- **Global Discounts**: store-wide discounts for followers, subscribers and groups. "Adding groups will supersede the defaults and be prioritized based on order from top to bottom. Adjust group order by dragging and dropping."
- **Fan Discounts**: per-fan discounts, which "will supersede the global video store discounts"
- **Video Discounts Summary**: discounts set on individual videos (from the video store). This page lists the discounts added per video
