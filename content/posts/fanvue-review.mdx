## How Fanvue Payouts Work (Step by Step)
 
This is where Fanvue is more transparent than most — its payout policy is published openly.
 
![Fanvue review payout timeline from sale to bank showing hold period, requirements and processing times](/images/fanvue-payout-timeline.png)
 
Here's the actual flow from a fan's payment to your bank:
 
1. **A fan pays** — subscription, tip, PPV message, or unlock.
2. **Fanvue takes its commission**, and the rest lands in your pending balance.
3. **The pending period runs.** Standard hold is **7 days**, but Fanvue's policy allows this to extend up to **28 days** depending on account verification status and activity, as determined by its fraud and risk systems. In practice, established verified accounts sit at the standard hold; new or flagged accounts can wait longer.
4. **You request a withdrawal** once your available balance reaches the **$20 minimum** — but only after you've met two other conditions: **full identity verification** (government ID + selfie, and verification for anyone who appears in your content) and **at least 5 pieces of content uploaded**.
5. **Processing takes roughly 3–7 business days** for a typical payout. Expect your **first payout to run longer — around 7–10 days** — because it triggers an additional fraud check.
6. **Choose your method:** bank transfer, supported eWallets, and crypto options are available, with fees varying by method and region. Standard domestic bank transfers are the default most creators use.

**What this means in practice:** from your first sale to money in your bank, plan for **2–3 weeks the first time**, and roughly weekly-to-biweekly rhythm after that. That's competitive — faster than platforms with long holds, slower than instant-payout fintech expectations. If cash flow in week one matters to you, know this going in.
 
**Payout comparison at a glance:** OnlyFans has the same $20 minimum with 3–5 day processing after its hold; Fansly processes fast but requires a much higher $100 minimum. Fanvue sits comfortably in the middle — low minimum, moderate speed, transparent policy.
