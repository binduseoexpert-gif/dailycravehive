## Set A Pay Price

When you add media to a post or a message, official help says you will see **Limit who can see this**. "This is where you set what a fan must do to unlock your content." To set a purchase price, official help gives two steps: tick **Pay**, then enter the **Amount**.

Official "Minimum Price of a Media Sale" help publishes the range as a minimum of $1.00 and a maximum of $500. "These limits apply to individual pieces of media you sell on the platform (such as photos and videos)." The Locked Media article says the same range in shorter form: "You can set a price anywhere between $1 and $500." Official help does not publish a suggested price inside that range, and a listed price is not a promise of sales.

You can also combine Pay with other permission options. Official help gives two examples. Tick **Subscribe** and **Pay** "so only subscribers can buy the media." Or click **Make it free for subscribers instead** "to let subscribers in for free while everyone else pays." Those are selling choices, not a full permissions tutorial. Official help also points to its Advanced Media Permissions FAQ if you want different prices for different fans, including one price for subscribers and another for everyone else. If a fan cannot see media after they pay or subscribe, use our Fansly subscriber cannot see content guide rather than changing the price first.

Official "Media Sales on Fansly" help restates the same Pay step, then adds what happens on the buyer's side: "When a user purchases your media, it is automatically unlocked for them." "Purchased media is stored securely in the buyer's Media Collection for future access." Official help in these articles does not say whether a sale can be refunded from Sale Stats, or how long media stays in that collection.

## Sell PPV In A Post

Official help says selling PPV in a post "is a great way to offer content to a wide audience." You can "organize it by adding it to a specific wall or pinning it to your wall so it's the first thing visitors see." Official help does not require a pin or a special wall, and it does not publish a limit on how many PPV posts you can keep pinned.

For more reach, official help says to enable the **Post to FYP** option "to appear in Fansly's For You Page." That is the official PPV article's only FYP step. If you want a locked post to appear there, our how to promote Fansly guide covers the official For You Page rule that a free preview is needed. This article does not repeat that FYP walkthrough.

Official "Media Sales on Fansly" help adds one more official writing tip: "Use clear titles and engaging thumbnails for your paid content to help increase visibility and drive more purchases!" That is official help's wording, not a test result from this site. Official help does not publish a required title format or a thumbnail size.

## Sell PPV In DMs And Mass Messages

Official help says selling in DMs "works very similarly to selling on your feed." Start in your inbox. You can "either send content directly to one person or send out a mass message to a group (or all) of your fans."

To message an existing fan, official help says to search for their display name in the **Search Conversations** bar or locate them in the list. "You'll find the upload icon in the bottom left corner of your thread." Alternatively, click the **envelope icon**. Official help places that icon above the Messages list on desktop computers, and in "the blue circle in the bottom right corner" on mobile browsers.

From the envelope menu, official help says you can send new messages, craft mass messages, or check previously scheduled messages with the **Scheduled** button "on the bottom left of the pop-up box." To craft a mass message, click **New Mass Message**. "From here, you can customize who the message is sent to and when." Official help then says the pricing process matches the feed: "Once you've selected who you want your message to go to, it follows the exact same process as when we sold content on the feed." Tick **Pay**, enter the **Amount**, and use the same subscriber options if you need them.

This page does not walk through every mass-message audience or scheduling control. Official help has a separate mass-message guide for those options. Keep the two jobs apart: here, you are attaching locked media and setting a Pay price. A trigger-based automated message is also a different official tool, and it is not required to sell PPV in a post or a DM.

## Read Sale Stats And Broadcast Message Stats

Official help treats stats as the way "to understand what's working for you." You can "check how well your mass messages perform and how well your content is selling."

For a piece of media, official help says to click the **three blue dots** in the top-right corner of the media, then select **Sale Stats**. You will see three statistics:

- **Total Sales**: how many times the media was purchased
- **Total Sales Value**: total revenue generated
- **Pending Sales Value**: earnings still in the 7-day processing period

Official help does not define "total revenue generated" as take-home pay after fees, and these PPV articles do not publish a media-sale fee. Read Total Sales Value as the official sales figure on that screen, not as a withdrawal balance. Pending Sales Value is officially limited to earnings still in the 7-day processing period. Official help does not say what happens to that figure on day eight, other than that it is no longer pending on this list.

To track a mass message, official help says to go to Creator Dashboard, Messages, then **Broadcast Message Stats**. There you will find:

- **Sent**: how many fans the message was sent to
- **Viewed**: how many fans opened the message
- **Purchases**: how many fans bought media from the message
- **Earned**: total income earned from the message, "if applicable"

Official help also says this is "a great place to check what media you included in the message." Official help does not publish a minimum open rate or a required purchase count. Use the official labels, and confirm the current dashboard path if the Messages menu uses a different name.
