## First, What Is LoyalFans?
 
LoyalFans is a subscription platform where fans pay creators directly — through monthly subscriptions, tips, paid messages, pay-per-view content, live streams, and a video store where you can sell individual clips.
 
If you know OnlyFans, the basic idea is the same. The difference is personality: LoyalFans tries to be a full "ecosystem" for creators. It gives you more built-in ways to earn from one fan — especially chatting and live features — and it positions itself as a cleaner, more professional space. It launched in 2020, it's free to join as a creator, and everyone must pass ID verification before selling anything.
## What Does LoyalFans Cost You? (Fees in Plain Numbers)
 
Money first, because this is what most people came for:
 
- **Joining:** free. No signup fee, no monthly seller fee.
- **The cut:** LoyalFans keeps **20%** of everything you earn. You keep **80%**. This applies to subscriptions, tips, paid messages, PPV, live-stream earnings — all of it.
- **Is 80/20 good?** It's the industry standard. OnlyFans and Fansly take the exact same cut. So nobody should pick or reject LoyalFans because of the fee — on price, the big platforms are a tie. (We compare the whole field in our [OnlyFans alternatives ranking](/onlyfans-alternatives).)
A worked example so the math is real: a fan subscribes at $10/month, tips you $5, and buys a $15 video. That's $30 gross. LoyalFans takes $6. **You keep $24.** Every platform review should show you this math; most don't.
 
**One bonus stream worth knowing:** LoyalFans runs a referral program that pays you **5% of the earnings of creators you refer** to the platform. If you have creator friends or an audience of sellers, this quietly becomes passive income on top of your own sales.
 
## How Do LoyalFans Payouts Work?
 
This is where LoyalFans is different from the platforms we've reviewed before — it runs on a **calendar rhythm**, not an "anytime withdrawal" model:

![LoyalFans payout cycle showing twice-monthly payment periods, processing window and $50 minimum](/images/loyalfans-payout-cycle.png)
1. **You earn** through the month — subs, tips, PPV, messages, streams.
2. **Payment periods close twice a month**, around the 1st and the 15th.
3. **Money is released within about 7 working days** after each period closes.
4. **You need at least $50** in your balance. Below $50? It rolls over to the next cycle — you don't lose it, you just wait.
5. **Payout methods:** ACH/direct deposit, wire transfer, SEPA (Europe), and Paxum, depending on your region.
**What this feels like in real life:** you get paid on a schedule, like a twice-monthly salary, instead of pressing a withdraw button whenever you want. Some creators love the predictability. Others miss the flexibility — OnlyFans, for comparison, has a lower $20 minimum and more withdrawal control.
 
**Honest note for beginners:** the $50 minimum stings most in month one, when earnings are small. If your first two weeks bring in $35, that money waits for the next cycle. It's not lost — but plan your expectations around it. (Fansly's minimum is even higher at $100, so LoyalFans sits in the middle of the field — see our [Fansly vs OnlyFans breakdown](/fansly-vs-onlyfans) for how the whole payout landscape compares.)
 
## The Chat & Messaging Features (LoyalFans' Real Strength)
 
People specifically search for reviews of LoyalFans' chatting features, and there's a good reason: **messaging is where subscription money is actually made**, and LoyalFans treats chat as a first-class tool instead of an afterthought.
 
Here's what the messaging toolkit gives you, in plain terms:
 
- **Paid messages:** you can charge for messages fans open — turning your inbox into a store, not just a chat window.
- **Mass messaging with pricing:** send one PPV message to many fans at once. This is the single biggest income lever on any subscription platform, and LoyalFans handles it smoothly.
- **Flexible tipping inside chat:** fans can tip mid-conversation without leaving the thread — small friction removed, real money added.
- **Shout-outs and custom requests:** fans can pay for personalized content directly, which feeds the custom-order pipeline that separates high earners from everyone else.
Why does this matter so much? Because on every fan platform we've reviewed, the pattern is the same: **the feed gets fans in the door; the DMs pay the rent.** Tips and PPV messages routinely out-earn base subscriptions for active creators. A platform that makes chatting easy to monetize is quietly handing you its most valuable feature.
 
**The honest catch:** good chat tools reward creators who actually reply. If you're not planning to spend time in your inbox — answering fans, sending PPV drops, closing custom requests — LoyalFans' biggest advantage goes unused. The tools don't chat for you. This matters more than it sounds: it means LoyalFans can work a little like a *marketplace* (sell one item to one buyer) on top of the subscription model. Clip sellers specifically should have LoyalFans on their shortlist for this feature alone.
 
## Is LoyalFans Legit? (The Trust Check)
 
Yes — LoyalFans is a legitimate, established platform. The signals that matter:
 
- Operating since 2020 with continuous payouts to creators
- Mandatory ID verification for every creator (and everyone appearing in content) — annoying at signup, protective forever
- All payments on-platform with published payout terms
- Active moderation and copyright protection — the platform has a reputation for being stricter about stolen content than most, which protects *you* as a creator
**Now the honest negatives, because "legit" doesn't mean flawless.** Looking across creator reports and reviews from 2026, three complaints come up repeatedly:
 
1. **Support is the weak spot.** Slow replies and unhelpful responses are the most common criticism. If something goes wrong with your account or a payout, expect friction. For a platform handling your income, this is a real minus — it's why Support gets a C on our report card.
2. **The audience is smaller.** LoyalFans has real, paying buyers — but far fewer of them than OnlyFans. Like most alternatives, it can't hand you traffic at OnlyFans scale. You'll still be doing your own promotion.
3. **Occasional payout friction reports.** A minority of creators report payment or withdrawal issues. Most payouts run on schedule — but keep records (screenshots of your dashboard at each period close) so any dispute is easy to document. That habit costs nothing and has saved creators real money.
None of these are scam signals — they're the normal rough edges of a mid-sized platform. But you deserve to know them before you build here.

## Getting Started: A Simple First Week
 
If you decide to try it, here's the first week without overwhelm:
 
**Day 1 — Verify.** Sign up free, submit ID verification (you and anyone who'll appear in content). Nothing sells until this clears, so do it first.
 
**Day 2 — Set up the money.** Add your payout method in settings now, not after your first sale. Confirm the current minimum and schedule while you're there.
 
**Days 3–4 — Stock the shelf.** Upload 10–15 varied pieces before promoting anything: feed posts, a few PPV-ready items, and 2–3 clips for the video store. An empty page converts nobody. (Our [content selling guide](/how-to-sell-feet-pics-and-make-money) covers building a starter catalog that converts, and it applies to any platform.)
 
**Day 5 — Price simply.** A modest subscription ($5–10 to start), clips at $10–20, customs listed at 2–3× your clip price. You'll adjust everything later with real data — day-five pricing just needs to be reasonable, not perfect.
 
**Days 6–7 — Open the inbox and promote.** Welcome-message every new fan, answer everything within hours, and start daily promotion wherever your audience lives. Then keep that rhythm for 90 days before judging results — every platform review we write ends with this advice because it's the variable that actually decides income.
 
## Safety Basics (Same Rules, Every Platform)
 
Quick but non-negotiable, whichever platform you pick:
 
- **Payment on-platform, always.** Anyone pushing you to PayPal, Venmo or crypto "to skip fees" is where scams live.
- **Payment before customs, every time.** No exceptions, no matter how nice the buyer seems.
- **Anonymity is fine and normal.** Verification is private — fans never see your ID. Stage name, no face, no identifying backgrounds if you prefer. It doesn't reduce earnings.
- **Taxes are real.** Creator income is taxable self-employment income. Track every payout from day one and set aside 20–30%.
- **Keep records.** Screenshot your balance at each payout period close. Boring habit, real protection.
