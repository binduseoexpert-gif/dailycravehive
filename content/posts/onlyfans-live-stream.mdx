## When Can You Go Live?

The Creator Center is explicit: Go Live is **not** on day one.

> "The ability to go live on OnlyFans is only enabled after a creator has demonstrated a history of providing great content, and a high level of engagement with their community. Once you have achieved this, the 'Go Live' feature will appear on your page."

The Go Live blog repeats that streaming appears after your profile has been active with a history of sharing great Content in keeping with the Terms.

OnlyFans does not publish a hard public number on that blog for "X posts and Y subscribers." Creators **commonly report** needing about five recent posts and five active subscribers plus feed activity before the button appears. Treat that as commonly reported and wait for **Go Live** on your own page rather than counting calendar days.

## Gear And Connection

From OnlyFans' Go Live guide:

- A **smartphone or computer** with camera and mic is enough
- Streams can be **vertical or horizontal**
- Prefer **strong Wi‑Fi**; ethernet on a computer is ideal. Mobile data works but is riskier
- Grant browser permissions for camera and microphone when prompted

Connection quality shows as a circle during the stream:

- **Red** - weak (bad viewer experience)
- **Yellow** - average
- **Green** - strong enough for high-quality streaming

## How To Start A Livestream (Official Steps)

OnlyFans' blog lists this browser flow:

1. Open OnlyFans in a recommended browser (Safari on iPhone/iPad; Chrome on Android; see OnlyFans' desktop browser list for computers)
2. Select the **Go Live** icon
3. Under **Available to**, choose which subscribers can join and whom to exclude (Lists help here - Features guide)
4. Write a short **description**
5. **Name your price** if you have a free profile - Live Video Price from **$5**
6. Add a **tip goal** and optional reward; choose whether the goal is visible
7. Choose whether viewers see **collected tips**
8. Press **Start Live Video**

Keep the connection indicator in view for the whole session.

## PPV Streams (Free Accounts)

The Creator Center and Features guide say **PPV streams** (ticketed lives) are for **free accounts** that are eligible to go live. Think of them as selling tickets. Ticket prices can start as low as **$5**. Paid subscription accounts still tip and engage on lives; the paid-entry stream tool is the free-account extra.

PPV **messages** are a separate product available to all creators; this article stays on live video.

## Co-Streaming

Official steps (Go Live blog + Creator Center):

- Both creators should be **subscribed** to each other
- Host: Settings under **Streaming** must allow co-stream requests from subscribers; start the live; invite via the three dots next to the guest's name, or accept their request
- Guest: join the host's stream as a viewer, then request to join or accept an invite
- Viewers can tip one or both creators
- Features guide: streamed videos land in the **Vault** for later VOD

If anyone else appears in explicit recorded Content later, co-author tagging and consent rules in the Terms still apply.

## Schedule Ahead With The Queue

OnlyFans recommends putting lives in your **Queue** so fans get an automatic reminder as start time approaches:

1. Choose date and time
2. Add cover image and stream info
3. Schedule
4. Return to the Queue at the set time and press **Go Live**

Starting late after a reminder burns trust faster than skipping an unannounced stream.

## Streaming With OBS

OnlyFans states OBS is compatible. Official outline:

1. Download OBS Studio from the OBS project site
2. Allow camera/mic; optimise for streaming
3. On OnlyFans: **Settings → Streaming** → copy the stream key under OBS software settings
4. In OBS: choose OnlyFans.com as Service, paste the key
5. Add video/audio sources
6. Start streaming in OBS
7. On OnlyFans Streaming page, open **Link stream to chat** to set tip goal, description and tip visibility
8. Keep OBS and OnlyFans open side by side so you can run the show and answer chat

Never share your stream key. Anyone with it can broadcast as you.

## Safety, Privacy And Terms While Live

- **Background:** remove mail, IDs, street signs, other people's faces without consent
- **Watermarking** still matters for clips that leave the stream; enable it in Privacy and safety (Creator Center)
- **Meetups:** Creator Center says in-real-life meetups between creators and fans are strictly prohibited
- **Content rules:** the Creator Center's removal list (unverifiable ages, under-18 role play, public nudity, weapons, illegal drugs, missing collab tags, and more) applies on camera too
- **Account risk:** The October 2026 Terms allow suspension, paused User Payments and withheld earnings during reviews if OnlyFans thinks you have or may have breached the Terms, for serious attempt/threat of breach, suspected unlawful or fraudulent earnings, actions reasonably likely to cause loss or harm reputation, and/or failure to promptly provide requested information. Dispute in writing within six months of Notification or you waive that right. Source: OnlyFans Terms of Service, Last updated: October 2026. Details: [account suspended](/onlyfans-account-suspended)
- **Scams:** nobody needs your stream key or password to "boost" a live. See [OnlyFans scams](/onlyfans-scams)
