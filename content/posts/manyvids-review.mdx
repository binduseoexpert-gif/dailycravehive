## Fees: Why "60%" And "80%" Both Appear

ManyVids' commission article is explicit: **percentages vary by service or product**, are **subject to change**, and should be re-checked on the official Help article or profile.

### Current creator payout rates (Help article, updated 1 July 2026)

| Service type | Creator payout |
|---|---|
| Club | 80% |
| Custom Vids | 80% |
| Tips | 80% |
| Store Items | 80% |
| Tokens (MV Live) | $0.08 per token* |
| Vids | 60% |
| Vid Bundles | 60% |
| Contest Votes | 60% |
| Pay To Open (inbox) | 60% |

\* Help text: the $0.08 token rate reflects the promotional payout rate implemented for MV Live's 80% payout structure.

So the headline "ManyVids takes 40%" is true **for standard vids** (creator keeps 60%). The headline "ManyVids is 80/20 like OnlyFans" is true **for Club, customs, tips and store items** — and false if most of your revenue is catalogue clip sales.

ManyVids' own explanation for keeping a share lists payment processing, fraud prevention, infrastructure, support, R&D, marketing and staffing (same Help article).

## Payouts: Wednesdays, $50 Minimum, 7 Days In Arrears

From ManyVids **Payout Information** (updated 1 July 2026):

- Payouts go out **each week on Wednesday** if you meet the minimum
- Earnings accrue **Wednesday 00:00 – Tuesday 11:59 UTC**
- Payouts are held **7 days in arrears** (you are paid a week after the week the earnings were made)
- Holiday payout dates move to the following business day
- You generally cannot change payout method in a locked window from **23:45 UTC Tuesday to 23:45 UTC Wednesday**

### Payment methods (official table)

| Method | Processing time | Minimum earnings | Fees |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct Deposit | 5 business days | $50.00 | USD 2.00 (US) / USD 5.50 (international) |
| Paxum Prepaid Cards | 3 business days | $50.00 | USD 1.00 transfer fee |
| Cosmopayment Prepaid Cards | 3 business days | $50.00 | Fees set by Cosmopayment |

Direct deposit is listed for a long set of countries (Canada, US, UK, Australia, much of the EU, Mexico, New Zealand and others — see the Help chart). **US citizens or anyone receiving US-bank direct deposit must submit a W-9** before adding that method, per the same article.

Most payouts arrive within about **2–7 business days** after release, depending on method. If a payout is late past normal processing, ManyVids points creators to its "Why haven't I received my Payout yet?" Help article.

## Who ManyVids Is For

**Strong fit when:**

- You already think in **catalogue clips** (scenes, themed vids, bundles) rather than only a monthly feed
- You want **customs** and **tips** on the 80% rows of the card
- Weekly Wednesday payouts with a clear $50 floor match your cash-flow planning
- You are willing to track **which product** each sale used so you understand your blended rate

**Weaker fit when:**

- Almost all revenue would be **à-la-carte vids** and you are comparing against OnlyFans' flat 80% creator share after the 20% User Payment fee
- You need a single subscription app experience and do not want to learn Club + store + contests
- You expected one marketing slogan ("60%" or "80%") to describe every line item

## Legit Platform, Still A Scam Surface

ManyVids is a long-running commercial site with public Help documentation for commissions and payouts. That does not make every DM that mentions ManyVids safe.

Watch for:

- Fake "ManyVids support" password resets
- Buyers demanding off-platform payment "to skip fees"
- Agencies that require upfront training fees before "managing your MV"

Phishing patterns are covered by the FTC's guide to [recognizing and avoiding phishing scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-recognize-and-avoid-phishing-scams).
