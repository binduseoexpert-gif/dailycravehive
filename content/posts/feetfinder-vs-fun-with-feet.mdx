## Safety, Privacy & Verification
 
Money aside, safety is where sellers can lose the most. Here's how the two compare.
 
### ID Checks
 
**FeetFinder** requires government ID from both sellers and buyers, and buyers must be verified before they can message sellers. That helps cut down on fake accounts and bots. It's run by FLRT Inc., a US company, and has operated since 2019.
 
**Fun With Feet** verifies sellers with government ID and doesn't share that information with buyers. Its site footer lists GYCO Online Limited, based in London, as the operator.
 
### Selling Without Showing Your Face
 
You can sell on both platforms without ever showing your face or using your real name. Your ID goes to the platform for verification and isn't shown to buyers. To stay anonymous in practice:
 
- Use a stage name and a separate email you don't use anywhere else.
- Crop out faces, tattoos, birthmarks and jewelry you wear often.
- Check backgrounds for mail, windows, room details or reflections.
- Strip location data (EXIF) from photos before uploading.
- Keep every conversation and payment inside the platform.
Our guide on [how to take feet pics that sell](https://www.dailycravehive.com/how-to-take-feet-pics) covers setups that look good without giving anything away.
 
### Scams & Chargebacks
 
The most common scams in this niche are buyers asking to pay outside the platform (gift cards, Cash App, "I'll send extra"), fake "verification fee" messages, and requests for free samples to "check quality". The rule is simple: never take payment or send content outside the platform.
 
On **chargebacks**, the two differ. FeetFinder's terms allow chargebacks and refunds to be taken from a seller's balance. Fun With Feet's seller FAQ says sellers are not liable for on-platform chargebacks; confirm exact exceptions in terms. For sellers doing higher-value custom work, that's a real point in Fun With Feet's favor.
