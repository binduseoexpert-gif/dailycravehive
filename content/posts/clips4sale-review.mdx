## What Is Clips4Sale?

Clips4Sale's help center describes the deal simply: if you're "a content creator or authorized content re-seller, Clips4Sale.com can securely host your media and enable you to open a store to offer it for sale." Approved creators upload media to their store, and once "copyright and quality control editors" approve it, customers can buy and download it.

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clips4Sale) describes C4S as an online marketplace for adult video specialising in fetish and kink content, founded in 2003 and owned by Centro Ventures since 2021. It says the service is operated in the US by Tropical Sun Corp. and in the EU and UK by Tropical Sun Ltd. (Cyprus). It also notes that in July 2026 Clips4Sale launched a **C4S+** subscription, expanding beyond single clip sales. We couldn't confirm C4S+ creator terms on the help articles we read, so check the official site for how it pays.

The key difference from a subscription site: on Clips4Sale, customers mostly **buy individual clips**. Your store is a catalogue, and each sale is paid separately.

## Payouts: Monthly On The 7th

From the help center:

- "Payouts are monthly and happen on the **7th of each month** for the previous month's sales revenue."
- You must meet your store's minimum payout. If you don't, "it carries over into the next month until you do."
- Minimums start at **$50** (US and US territories), **$100** (Canada) and **$150** (international).
- Payout methods listed: **Bank Deposit, Wire Transfer, Crypto, Paxum or Check**.

### What Monthly Payouts Mean For Cash Flow

A clip sold on November 2 is part of November's revenue and is paid on **December 7** (if you've met the minimum). That's a wait of up to five weeks or more. If you need weekly cash, a monthly clip store works better as a second income stream than as your only one.

Per-method transfer fees aren't listed on the help articles we read. Third-party reviews **commonly report** that US ACH is free and wires carry a fee. Check the payout options in your store before you choose a method.
