## How MYM Says The Product Works

Official help says members "can subscribe to one or more Creators to access private galleries that are regularly updated." "Upon subscription, the blurred content of the Creator's feed becomes accessible." The creator sets the cost, and official help names two fan offers: a monthly subscription or a biannual subscription.

Once a fan is subscribed, official help says they "can then engage in conversations and make requests for personalized content to the creators, who are free to accept by offering a price, or refuse." Official introduction help also says you can send push media that earns money each time it is unlocked. The request and delivery steps, including the official 72-hour window, are covered in our private media requests guide linked above. Official introduction help lists payouts "via Bank Transfer or PayPal." **Note a conflict:** official SuperStar help (edited October 14, 2026) lists "The ability to receive transfers from your MYM balance to a PayPal account" as a SuperStar benefit. The two official pages do not say the same thing about who can use PayPal, so check the options in your own Payment methods settings before you count on it.

## What Official Help Says You Keep

Official "MYM fees" help says fees are calculated with VAT, bank charges, and fee rates. Bank charges come first: "Bank charges represent 7% of the amount of a transaction (taxes included)." After that deduction, MYM applies a payout percentage "on the price of the purchase excluding taxes." Official help lists these creator shares:

- 75% on subscriptions
- 80% on private media
- 80% on private show
- 80% on tips

"The balance represents the processing fees for hosting and all costs related to the operation of the MYM site." For SuperStar creators, official help says the share is "+1% higher on private media and subscriptions," with tips still at 80%. Official SuperStar help says the status is assigned on the 1st of each month, for one month, based on the previous month: a certified account, no warning, regular log-ins, at least 50 active subscribers throughout the month, at least two feed posts a week, and requests answered with private media delivered within 48 hours on average.

VAT is separate from the 7% bank charge. Official help says that for subscribers in a European Union country, "VAT is added to the price of a subscription, private media or tip and is paid by the subscriber. This sum is entirely paid to the State." Official help also says "your repayment could vary slightly depending on the geographical location of the Fan who made the purchase." If you use a professional account in France and are subject to VAT, official help says the VAT must be added when you send your invoice. That is not tax advice.

### The Official 100 Euro Example

Official help gives one worked repayment example for a private-media sale you set at 100 euros:

- If the subscriber is in France: "Price paid by the subscriber: 100 euros + 20% VAT = 120 euros." "Bank charges: 120 euros x 7% = 8.40 euros." "Your repayment calculation: (100 euros - 8.40) x 80% = 73.28 euros."
- If the subscriber is in the United States: "Price paid by the subscriber (no VAT): 100 euros." "Bank charges: 100 euros x 7% = 7 euros." "Your repayment calculation: (100 euros - 7) x 80% = 74.40 euros."

These are official examples, not a promise of earnings. The 80% share is applied after the bank charge, which is why a 100 euro private-media sale does not pay 80 euros in the official example.

## Pending Earnings, Then Validated Earnings

Official help distinguishes two balance states:

- "Pending" earnings: "recent earnings from your balance which, for safety reasons, cannot be withdrawn during a given period."
- "Validated" earnings: "earnings that have successfully passed the validation period, which can be transferred to the payment method of your choice."

Official help publishes these automatic validation periods:

- Subscription earnings: 15 days on monthly subscriptions, and 60 days on six-month subscriptions
- Private or push media, and private show: 7 days. "For Private media, your balance is credited once the media has been delivered. For private show, your balance is credited once the live has been finished."
- Tips: 7 days

"In the case of a Fan transaction with a suspicion of fraud, these periods may exceptionally be extended." Official help adds that tip payments also arrive as a blue direct message signed MYM. The withdrawal minimum is counted in validated earnings, not pending ones, so a 50 euro official minimum can take longer to reach than 50 euros of recent sales.

## How Withdrawals Work

Official help lists three conditions "in order for your payments to be made securely":

- Have a certified account
- Have added a method of payment, in Account Settings, Payment methods
- Have 50 euros of validated earnings, or "100 euros of validated earnings for creators with non-SEPA RIBs including SWIFT"

"All transfers not categorized as SEPA are SWIFT transfers." Official help publishes this SEPA country list: Austria, Belgium, Cyprus, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Portugal, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Bulgaria, Croatia, Czech Republic, Denmark, Hungary, Poland, Romania, Sweden, United Kingdom, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, Monaco, Switzerland, and San Marino.

For professional accounts, official help adds a fourth condition: "you will have, in addition to these 3 conditions, to upload a monthly invoice to your account (in your settings, section 'Payments received') in order to unblock your payment."

### Payment Schedule

If you meet those conditions, official help says payments "will be automatically generated according to the period of your choice," except for professional accounts that are waiting on an invoice. The official frequency choices are twice a week, once a week, twice a month, or once a month. "As a reminder, payments are made every Monday and Thursday morning. Payments are also triggered on the 1st and 15th of each month, even if these dates do not correspond to your usual payment days." Professional invoices "are reviewed every Monday and Thursday before 10:00 AM. Any invoice uploaded after 10:00 AM will be reviewed during the next session, and the payment will therefore be postponed to the next payment date." On a public holiday, official help says payment is made the following day if banking services are not operational. Official help does not publish a transfer-arrival time.

## Certification, The Ambassador Program, And Trust

Official certification help says: "A certified account allows you to obtain a badge next to your username and to be able to receive your payments securely." It also says, "You will only have a profile that is really visible to fans once you are certified on MYM." So certification is both a payout condition and a visibility condition.

Official help also describes an Ambassador, or sponsorship, program. Official sponsorship help says you earn "10% income from all the Creators who sign up on MYM thanks to your personal sponsorship link, on private media, push media, private show and subscriptions," and that tips are not included. The same article says commission collection is "now limited to 2 years" from the referred account's creation, and that no reward is paid when the buyer used a MYM promo code.
