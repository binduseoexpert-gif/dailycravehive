## Step 1: Choose Broadcasting Software

Fansly "doesn't endorse any specific platform," but its Start Live Streaming guide uses **OBS Studio** throughout and links to OBS's own Quickstart Guide.

**Desktop:** Download OBS Studio and follow OBS's setup for scenes, mic and camera.

**Mobile:** Fansly says to use an app that supports **RTMP ingest**. The official guide names **PRISM** (iOS and Android) and **Streamlabs** (iOS and Android) as popular options. Creator Hub also treats OBS as the main example.

You don't need a fancy overlay on day one. A stable camera, clear audio and the right server/key settings are enough to go live.

## Step 2: Generate Your Stream Key (And Keep It Private)

Your Stream Key links your software to your Fansly account. Fansly says: "Think of it like your stream's private passcode: it's essential for connecting, and it should never be shared."

To generate it:

1. Open your Fansly **Streaming Settings** (Creator Dashboard → Streaming).
2. Click **Generate Stream Key**.

**Security rules from Fansly:**

- Never share the key with anyone.
- If someone else gets access, **reset it immediately** in Streaming Settings.
- Creator Hub adds: reset the key if you suspect it's compromised, and keep it updated when you change devices.

**Practical habits (editorial, not Fansly quotes):**

- Don't paste the key into public chats, Discord tickets or "manager" DMs.
- Don't leave the OBS Settings → Stream window on screen while you're live, the key can appear in your own broadcast.
- Never show a real stream key in a screenshot or tutorial image. Use a blurred or placeholder string.
- After a reset, paste the **new** key into OBS or your mobile app before the next stream.

Fake "tech support" that asks for your stream key is a scam. Our [OnlyFans scams](/onlyfans-scams) guide covers the phishing scripts that hit creators across platforms.

## Step 3: Connect OBS (Or Your Mobile App)

From Fansly's Start Live Streaming guide:

1. In OBS, go to **Settings → Stream**.
2. Select **Custom** as your server/service.
3. Enter both your **Stream Server** and **Stream Key** from Fansly Streaming Settings.

On a mobile RTMP app, the labels are usually the same idea: server URL + stream key. Paste both from Streaming Settings, not from a third-party "Fansly key" site.

Once this is saved correctly, Fansly says you can return to OBS later and just press **Start Streaming**.

## Step 4: Set Viewing Permissions Before You Go Live

In your Live Streaming Dashboard, Fansly's monetizing guide says to go to **Limit who can watch** and choose what fans must do to watch:

- **Follow you**
- **Subscribe**
- **Buy a ticket**
- **Have tipped you**

You can tick more than one, and fans must meet **all** of the ones you tick. For other options, such as letting fans in through a **private list**, click **Advanced**. Fansly also says to check the **Fans will see** preview to confirm your setup.

### The Empty-Permission Warning

The two official Help Center articles say the same thing in slightly different words:

- Start Live Streaming: **"If you don't choose any permissions, your stream will be accessible to any logged-in Fansly user."**
- Monetizing Your Live Streams: **"If nothing is ticked, everyone can watch."**

Either way, an empty setup is an open stream. Leave it empty only if you mean it.

| Viewing setup | Who can watch |
|---|---|
| Nothing ticked | Open: "any logged-in Fansly user" / "everyone" per Fansly's two guides |
| Buy a ticket | Fans who buy the ticket |
| Subscribe | Your subscribers |
| Several boxes ticked | Only fans who meet all of them |
| Private list (Advanced) | Only people on the selected list(s) |

Creator Hub's private-list tip: choose which List(s) get access, and "only users on those lists will be able to view or interact with the stream." That fits paid one-on-one sessions, VIP groups or pre-sale ticket lists.

Set viewing permissions **before** you start. Creators **commonly report** that mid-stream permission changes may not apply until you restart. Treat that as commonly reported, not an official promise.

## Step 5: Optional Chat Room, Goals And Title

### Chat Room

Enable a chat room with **"Generate chat room."** Chat has its **own** permissions, separate from viewing. In the Stream Chat pane, under **To message you, fans must**, Fansly's monetizing guide lists:

- **Follow you**
- **Subscribe**
- **Pay per message** (viewers pay a set amount for each message)
- **Have tipped you** (at least a set amount)
- **Followed by you** (only people you follow can chat)
- **Have bought media worth** (at least a set amount of your media)

As with viewing, ticking several means fans must meet all of them. Click **Advanced** to give fans a choice or to limit chat to a private list. You can also toggle **Hide Creator Messages**, **Show Gifs in Messages** and **Allow Private Chat**.

Creator Hub publishes chat commands for moderators, including `/MOD`, `/BAN`, `/TIMEOUT`, `/UNBAN`, `/SLOW` and `/SLOWOFF`. Brief a moderator before a busy stream if you use them.

### Stream Goal

Scroll to the bottom of Streaming Settings → **Create New Goal**. Enter:

- **Title**, short name
- **Description**, what the goal supports
- **Amount to Raise**, tip/contribution target

Fansly says the goal appears on the stream and updates in real time.

### Stream Title

Add a custom title so viewers know what the session is about, useful for themed or event streams.

### Lovense And Toys

Fansly supports Lovense when a chat room is generated. Toy integration is **outside the scope of this guide**, use Fansly's own Lovense help articles if you need that setup.

## Step 6: Start Streaming

When permissions, chat and goals look right:

1. Open OBS (or your RTMP app).
2. Click **Start Streaming**.
3. Confirm viewers can connect (Fansly has a related Help article on viewing your live-stream audience).

After the first successful setup, Fansly says you can simply return and press Start Streaming next time, as long as the key hasn't been reset and your permissions still match the audience you want.

## Content Rules While You're Live

Creator Hub's pro tip: keep content aligned with Fansly's guidelines and Terms of Service. "Avoid streaming restricted content, and make sure you have all the necessary rights for streamed material, especially when incorporating elements like background music or video game footage."

Don't stream anything you don't have rights to. Background music and game footage are common trip-ups.
