## Is Fun With Feet Safe? Safety, Privacy & Legality
 
Safety matters more than any earnings figure, so let's be thorough.
 
**On legality:** Selling feet content is legal in the United States and most countries, provided everyone involved is 18 or older and properly verified. That's why reputable platforms, Fun With Feet included, require age verification. Rules vary by location, so check your local laws.
 
**On platform safety:** Fun With Feet does the fundamentals right. It requires ID verification for sellers, handles payments on-platform, and keeps your identity private from buyers.
 
Whatever platform you choose, follow these safety rules:
 
- **You must be 18 or older.** No exceptions on any legitimate platform.
- **Protect your identity.** Never show your face, real name, identifying tattoos or recognizable backgrounds.
- **Watermark your previews.** Add a visible watermark to promo or preview images (not the final paid content) to discourage theft.
- **Keep payments on-platform.** Never let a buyer pull you off-platform to pay privately. That's where scams and chargebacks live.
- **Never send content before payment clears.** "Send it first, I'll pay after" is the oldest scam in the niche.
- **Strip photo metadata.** Images can carry EXIF location data. Remove it before uploading anywhere.
> **Safety bottom line:** Fun With Feet is a safe, verified platform when you follow standard precautions. Its verification and on-platform payments put it well ahead of selling through unverified social-media DMs.
