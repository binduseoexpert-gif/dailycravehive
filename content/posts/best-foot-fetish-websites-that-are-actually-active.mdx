## Red Flags: How to Spot a Dead or Scammy Platform
 
Not every site calling itself a "foot fetish website" deserves your time or your ID. Watch for these warning signs:
 
- **Upfront fees with no visible buyer activity.** If a platform wants membership money but you can't see any signs of real buyers, walk away.

- **Dead community signals.** Empty forums, no recent activity, broken links — signs the platform is a ghost town collecting fees.
The safest platforms are the ones with transparent costs, clear safety rules, real buyer discovery, and a payment flow you can understand *before* you upload anything.
