## Part 1: Fansly's Built-In Discovery Tools

### Profile Tags

According to Fansly's "Profile Discoverability Features" article, profile tags are "private labels you assign to your profile to help the algorithm understand what kind of content you create." Fansly says it uses them to match you with users who enjoy similar content and to place you in users' Suggestions tabs.

**How to use them well:** pick tags that accurately describe what you post. Accurate tags put you in front of fans who actually want your niche. Misleading tags get clicks from people who won't subscribe.

### Hashtags

The same article explains that hashtags "are public and appear in your post captions." They help posts appear in hashtag searches and on relevant pages. Fansly's own tip is to "combine niche and trending hashtags to maximize your reach." The [Wikipedia article on hashtags](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hashtag) gives useful background on how they work across platforms.

Fansly's recommendations article notes that "posts, hashtags, and emojis must comply with Fansly's Terms of Service." Stick to relevant, rule-compliant tags.

### The For You Page (FYP)

Fansly calls the FYP its "built-in discovery system." According to its "For You Page (FYP)" article, getting a post onto it involves a few steps:

1. **Add a free preview to locked media.** "Without a preview, your post won't appear on FYP."
2. **Select "Post to FYP"** when creating the post. If something's missing, a pop-up tells you what to fix.
3. **Check your timeline settings.** If your timeline is locked to followers or subscribers, Fansly says to turn on the "Internal discovery when the timeline is hidden" toggle in Account Settings. Alternatively, you can add #fyp to individual posts.

Fansly also notes that only posts you opt in are included, and that content marked private won't appear on the FYP.

**Posting only to the FYP:** Fansly explains that you can post to the FYP without showing the post on your profile. To do this, make your Posts wall private, choose a different main wall, then deselect all walls (or pick a private one) when you post. This is useful if you want a tidy profile but still want reach.

### Free Previews

Fansly's "Adding a Free Preview to Your Media" article explains that you choose part of your media to show as a teaser. The built-in editor lets you "blur, censor, or add text." Fansly suggests keeping previews short and experimenting with different ones. Because previews are required for the FYP, they're one of the most useful promotion tools you have.

### Connections

According to "Profile Discoverability Features," you can connect Twitch and X (formerly Twitter) accounts under Connections in Settings. Fansly says that if someone who follows you on Twitch or X connects their account or signs in with one of those, your profile may be recommended to them. Fansly also says it "will never post on your connected platforms or edit your information without your permission."

### Collaboration: Tagging And Reposting

Fansly's "Tagging System" article explains that typing @ plus a creator's name links to their profile. Its reposting article draws an important line:

- **Reposting** shares another creator's post with credit and needs no consent form.
- **Reuploading** someone else's content to your account requires signed consent forms. Failing to get consent "may lead to enforcement action against your account."

Fansly's advice is: "When in doubt, repost instead of reupload." Collaborating with creators in a similar niche is commonly reported to be one of the better ways to reach new fans.

## Part 2: Pricing Tools That Help Promotion

Fansly's "How Do I Offer a Discount on My Subscriptions?" article lists several options:

- **Promotional discounts:** a limited-time price that doesn't change your base price. Fansly says "your followers will be notified when a new promotion starts."
- **Multi-month discounts:** 2-, 3- or 6-month plans, with discounts of up to 50% off.
- **Subscription discount links:** special rates, or free access, for selected people.

According to the "Free Trials or Lifetime Subscriptions" article, free trials are available, but free lifetime subscriptions aren't. Use these tools sparingly. Constant discounts are commonly reported to train fans to wait for a sale.

## Part 3: Off-Platform Promotion

Fansly itself encourages promoting elsewhere. Its "Advertising on Social Media" article advises learning "best practices for each platform" and including "a direct link to your Fansly profile in your posts or bio." It also warns: "Before linking to any external sites, review the Terms of Service (TOS) for each platform to ensure your content complies with their guidelines."

That warning matters. Mainstream platforms have their own rules on adult content and links, and breaking them can cost you an account.

### Tips For Off-Platform Promotion

- **Use one consistent name.** Fansly's "Creating an Identity or Brand" article recommends using "the same username on all your accounts" so fans can find you.
- **Use a stage name if you want privacy.** The same article suggests one "if you prefer to protect your personal identity."
- **Keep teasers within each platform's rules.** Read the rules before you post. They're often stricter than Fansly's.
- **Add links to your Fansly bio.** According to "Promoting Other Websites," you can add links to X, Instagram, Linktree and more, "as long as they follow our Terms of Service."
- **Post consistently** rather than in bursts. This is commonly reported advice, not a Fansly rule.
- **Engage with fans.** Fansly's branding article recommends you "respond to comments and messages."

## Part 4: Measure What Works With Tracking Links

Fansly's "Tracking Links on Fansly" article explains that tracking links "show you which sources drive the most engagement and revenue." You can create a separate link for each platform, for example one for your Instagram bio.

Fansly also gives you three built-in internal tracking links: **FYP**, **Suggestions** and **Search**. These show how many fans find you inside Fansly itself.

According to the article, the metrics include:

- **Claims:** clicks that lead to a login or sign-up. Clicks from visitors who don't log in or sign up aren't counted.
- **Follows:** follows after clicking the link
- **Subscriptions:** new subscriptions from the link
- **Revenue:** total revenue from people who used the link

Fansly notes that tracking links are different from referrals. They measure performance rather than earning referral income directly.

**Practical tip:** check your links regularly. Put more effort into the sources that bring subscriptions, not just clicks.

## A Simple Weekly Promotion Routine

There's no official Fansly schedule, so treat this as a starting framework rather than a rule. It simply puts the tools above in order:

1. **Review your tracking links.** Note which sources brought follows and subscriptions, not just claims.
2. **Check your profile tags.** Make sure they still describe what you actually post.
3. **Prepare posts with free previews** and opt the right ones into the FYP.
4. **Share teasers off-platform** within each platform's rules, using the same username everywhere.
5. **Reply to comments and messages,** as Fansly's branding article recommends.
6. **Plan any promotion** in advance instead of discounting every week.

## Promotion Mistakes And Safety

- **Paying for "guaranteed growth."** Fansly's FYP article says its support team "is unable to help boost engagement or provide individualized advice." Be wary of anyone selling guaranteed placement or followers.
- **Agency and login scams.** Never share your Fansly login. Our [OnlyFans scams guide](/onlyfans-scams) covers agency and impersonation tricks that apply to Fansly creators too.
- **Phishing.** The FTC's guide to [recognizing and avoiding phishing scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-recognize-and-avoid-phishing-scams) advises contacting a company using a website you know is real, not links in unexpected messages.
- **Reuploading without consent.** Repost instead.
- **Ignoring other platforms' rules.** A banned promotion account means lost traffic.
