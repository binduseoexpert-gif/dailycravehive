## Is Feetify Safe? Safety, Privacy & Legality
 
Safety is where this review gets serious, because this is the area where Feetify's design choices create real risk for you. None of this makes Feetify a scam — but you need to understand these gaps before you list a single photo.
 
### The two biggest safety gaps
 
**1. No payment protection or escrow.** Unlike FeetFinder, which handles transactions on-platform and holds payment until both sides confirm, Feetify leaves the actual payment between you and the buyer. Most payments happen through PayPal, Cash App, or crypto. That gives you more control — but far less protection. There's no escrow, no built-in dispute resolution. **2. No ID verification at signup.** Feetify doesn't require government ID to create an account. That makes joining fast and easy — but it also means the platform can't guarantee who's on the other side of a conversation, and it removes a safety layer that verified platforms rely on. Platforms that verify both buyers and sellers create accountability; Feetify's open-door approach trades that away for convenience.
 
### Non-negotiable safety and legality rules
 
Whatever platform you choose, these rules protect you:
 
- **You must be 18 or older.** This is absolute. Every legitimate platform requires it, and letting anyone underage sell — or letting someone else use your profile — can result in permanent bans and serious legal consequences. Never compromise here.
- **Protect your identity.** Never show your face, real name, home, workplace, or any identifying tattoos or backgrounds in your content unless you fully understand the exposure. Anonymity is one of the biggest advantages of this niche — don't give it up carelessly.
- **Keep payments and conversations on-platform where possible.** The moment a buyer pushes you to move to a private channel or pay another way "to avoid fees," treat it as a red flag. Off-platform is exactly where scams and chargebacks live.
- **Never send content before you're paid** through a verified, traceable method. "Send it first and I'll pay right after" is the oldest scam in the niche.
- **Scrub metadata from your photos.** Images can carry location data (EXIF). Strip it before uploading anywhere.
> **Safety bottom line:** Feetify is safe *enough* to be legitimate, but its lack of escrow and signup verification puts more of the safety burden on you. A platform like FeetFinder, which verifies every user and protects payments on-platform, removes exactly the risks that hurt beginners most — which is a big part of why we recommend it as the safer starting point.
