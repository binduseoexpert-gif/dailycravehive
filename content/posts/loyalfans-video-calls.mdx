## This Is A Two-Way Call, Not A Live Broadcast

One on One is a one-to-one call. Official help says you and the fan can both be seen or heard, depending on whose camera is on. That is the opposite of official [Go Live help](https://loyalfans.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/10231272897052-Go-Live), which describes a cam show where fans see and hear you, and you do not hear or see your fans.

Official Go Live help also describes **ONLY PRIVATE** as a per-minute option "where viewers can pay per minute to watch an exclusive show or part of the show." That is still a live-show room, not a One on One call. If you want a paid one-to-one conversation, official help points you to One on One, not to Only Private. This guide does not repeat Go Live camera, audience, or tip-goal steps.

Official ["Timeline Stories and Highlights"](https://loyalfans.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/10228988026396-Timeline-Stories-and-Highlights) help, also last edited September 1, 2023, mentions One on One only as something you can advertise: "In Stories you can advertise your latest projects, latest updates, upcoming Go LIVE or ONE on ONES or any new updates on your profile." That is a promotion mention, not a second setup article. Confirm the current Stories labels if you use that path.

## Camera, Tips And The Credit Warning

You can take a call without turning your camera on. Official help says: "You do not have to turn on your camera to use this feature but it is recommended that you let your fans know that they won't be able to view it." That is an official wording recommendation, not a required script. If the camera will stay off, tell the fan before or at the start of the call so the two-way setup official help describes matches what they can actually see.

Official help also says: "Your fan will get notify before he runs out of credit before he gets disconnected." That sentence is quoted as the help article publishes it. Official help does not say how far in advance the warning appears, whether you see the same warning, or how unused credit is handled. It also does not describe a separate hang-up button for one call. The only stop method in this article is the availability toggle, covered below.

"Fans can also tip you during One on One audio or video calls." Official help does not publish a tip minimum, a tip fee, or a menu for those in-call tips. Treat in-call tips as an official option, and confirm the current tip control on the call screen. Official help does not say that a tip extends the call when credit is running out, so do not promise that.

## Toggle Off To Stop And Leave The Listing

Official help uses the same dashboard control to stop: "To stop your One on One audio or video call simply toggle OFF the feature in your dashboard to disconnect you and remove you from the general LoyalFans active CALL A CONTENT CREATOR listing."

Read that as two results from one action. Official help says toggling off disconnects you and removes you from the listing. It does not describe a way to end one call and stay listed, or to leave the listing while a call continues. If you only want to stop taking new calls, confirm whether the current dashboard has a control official help does not name. If it does not, toggling off is the official stop method, and it will also take you out of the listing.
