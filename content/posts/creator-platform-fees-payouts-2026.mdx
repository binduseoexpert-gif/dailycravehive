## What The Table Shows

**20% is the default on most adult subscription platforms.** OnlyFans, Fansly, Fanvue and LoyalFans keep 20% as standard. ManyVids is the outlier with a **per-product** card (60% or 80% to the creator depending on what sold). Patreon's standard creator plan is **10%**, plus separate processing and payout fees.

**Holds matter more than headline fees.** Fansly and ManyVids hold each sale about 7 days. Fanvue's policy allows a hold of up to 28 days. FeetFinder's terms set a **30-day review period** before payout. Patreon does not use the same multi-day sale hold model — you request a payout from your balance.

**Feet marketplaces trade commission for plan fees.** FeetFinder charges 10% on Basic and 0% on Premium on top of the plan price. Fun With Feet takes no commission but charges membership and has a $50 withdrawal floor. Feetify lets buyers pay you directly, so there is no platform payout and no buyer protection either.

For seller-side detail beyond the table, see our [FeetFinder review](/feetfinder-review), [Fansly review](/fansly-review) and [ManyVids review](/manyvids-review).

## Methodology

This table is based on each platform's **published terms, help centre and FAQ pages**, re-checked on **5 November 2026**. It is not based on account testing, and we did not run creator accounts to measure payouts. Where a platform does not publish a number, we write "not published" or mark a community figure as **commonly reported** rather than inventing a value.

Sources used for this update include OnlyFans Terms, Fansly Help Center, Fanvue Creator Earnings & Payouts Policy, LoyalFans Terms, ManyVids Creator Commission Payout Percentages (updated 1 July 2026) and Payout Information, Patreon Creator fees overview (updated September 2026), FeetFinder Terms (effective 30 September 2026), Fun With Feet seller FAQ, and the Feetify FAQ.

Platforms change their terms often. We'll update this page when an official page changes. If you spot an outdated figure, contact us and we'll check it.
