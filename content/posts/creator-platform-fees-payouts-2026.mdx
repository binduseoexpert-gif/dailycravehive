## What The Table Shows

**The 20% cut is the norm on subscription platforms.** OnlyFans, Fansly, Fanvue and LoyalFans all keep 20% as standard. The real differences are in the minimum payout and how long your money waits.

**Holds matter more than headline fees.** Fansly holds each sale for 7 days. Fanvue's policy allows a hold of up to 28 days, depending on its risk checks. FeetFinder's terms set a 30-day review period before payout. If you need money fast, compare these rows first.

**Feet marketplaces trade commission for fees.** FeetFinder charges 10% on Basic and 0% on Premium, on top of the plan price. Fun With Feet takes no commission but charges a membership and has a $50 withdrawal floor. Feetify lets buyers pay you directly, so there's no platform payout and no buyer protection either.

## Methodology

This table is based on each platform's **published terms, help centre and FAQ pages**, checked on 24 October 2026. It is not based on hands-on testing, and we didn't run creator accounts to measure payouts. Where a platform doesn't publish a number, we write "not published" rather than repeat figures from forums or other blogs.

Platforms change their terms often. OnlyFans' revised terms apply from 1 November 2026, and FeetFinder's current terms took effect on 30 September 2026. We'll update this page when an official page changes. If you spot an outdated figure, contact us and we'll check it.
