## Who Needs To Verify

Patreon says payment processors require it to verify that Adult/18+ creators sharing sexually explicit creations or nudity are over 18. The requirement "applies to creators in the Adult/18+ category."

You can **opt out of Age Verification** by recategorizing from Adult/18+ to **"Safe for All Audiences"** - but only if you remove every piece of sexually explicit content or nudity (or never posted any). Patreon links that path from the FAQ; follow the official recategorization steps if that's your choice.

**Participant consent is separate.** Patreon says you are responsible for obtaining written consent from any real humans who appear in your works, retaining that documentation indefinitely, and providing it if Patreon asks after a payment-partner request. That is not the same as your own age check.

## Path 1: Government ID + Selfie (Most Creators)

From the Age Verification FAQ:

1. Select your **country** - the process differs for some countries, including South Korea.
2. Submit a **government-issued identification** showing full name, birthdate and photo (license, passport or national ID).
3. Submit **photos of that document** to Patreon's third-party verification vendor.
4. Take a **selfie** in that moment; it is matched to the photo on the ID.

Patreon expects this to take about **3–5 minutes**. You can start from a desktop browser, mobile browser, or the iOS/Android apps. Progress **does not carry over** if you switch devices mid-process - finish on one device.

### Privacy Notes From Patreon

- Patreon says it selected a vendor with security certifications including ISO/IEC 27001:2013, PCI DSS and SOC2 Type 2.
- Information from verification is **not** used to update public account or campaign details.
- Patreon says it will **never publicly share or sell** personal information from the check, and will never publicly share the name from the ID you submit.
- If you want to **redact your address** from a government ID photo, Patreon says to reach out to them.

### Troubleshooting The Standard Path

From "ID verification troubleshooting tips" (Updated January 30, 2026):

- Use only **valid, non-expired** government ID showing full name, birth date and photo.
- If one ID fails, try another that clearly shows those same fields.
- On phone: latest Patreon app and latest device software.
- Or use a browser at patreon.com with a webcam / mobile browser camera.
- Good lighting; avoid shadows or glare on the ID.
- Resubmit from your **Notifications** page.
- If it still fails, submit a support request through Patreon.

## Path 2: South Korea Digital ID Verification

Because of South Korea's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), Patreon says it can no longer accept ID documents that contain a **Resident Registration Number (RRN)**.

South Korean creators verify with **Digital ID Verification**:

- A quick check through your **mobile carrier** or **credit card provider**
- **No selfie** and **no document upload**

This is **not** the alternative (non-government-issued) ID process. Patreon says the alternative-document pathway "is not available as a substitute for South Korean creators." If Digital ID Verification isn't working, contact Patreon support - don't mail in List A/B/C documents instead.

## Path 3: Alternative (Non-Government-Issued) IDs

### Who Is Eligible

You may use alternative IDs if you:

- Do **not** have a government-issued ID, **or**
- Have a **serious privacy concern** about using your government-issued ID

Patreon "strongly encourage[s]" creators who *do* have a government ID to use the secure Notifications-page flow instead. Alternative verification "may take **many months** depending on volume."

Patreon currently accepts alternative IDs **only** for Adult/18+ age verification. Creators asked to verify for another reason still need a government-issued ID.

To start the alternative path, Patreon says: "reach out to us." Additional emails are sent when Patreon is ready to accept documents. Submissions use **SendSafely** (end-to-end encryption). After verification, Patreon says documents are **permanently deleted within 14 days**, and only Trust & Safety accesses them.

### Document Rules

Every alternative document must:

- Be written in **English, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French or Italian**
- Be a clear photo or scan, no glare, all four corners visible, readable font
- Be valid as of the submission date if it has an expiration date
- Show the **exact same name** across documents (or include notarized name-change paperwork - e.g. marriage certificate, divorce decree, certificate of naturalization showing a new name, or a signed court order)

Collectively, the packet must show your **full name, date of birth and photo**.

### What To Submit

| List | How many | Must show |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | **1** | Name **and** date of birth |
| **B** | **2** (different types) | Name (plus listed fields per document) |
| **C** | **1**, held in a selfie/photo of you | Name **and** photo on the card |

**List A (pick one):** original/certified birth certificate; hospital record of birth; report of birth abroad; federal or state census record; final adoption decree; military record; record of naturalization.

**List B (pick two different types):** social security card or equivalent (name, letterhead, last 4 of SSN); voter ID; refugee/asylum ID; utility bill within 90 days; financial document within 90 days; most recent tax return; mortgage/lease/rental agreement; pay stub; insurance policy; education record (transcript/diploma). You may mask fields other than those Patreon says must stay visible. Two bills of the *same* type (two electric bills) are not allowed; two *different* utility types are fine.

**List C (hold one in a photo of yourself):** library card; student ID; employment ID; loyalty card; public transportation card; club/organization membership card; debit or credit card.

Name mismatches across lists need the notarized name-change paperwork noted above.

## New Pages And Transfers

If you open a **new** Adult/18+ page with sexually explicit content or nudity, Patreon says you complete verification again. Verification status **cannot be transferred** between accounts.
